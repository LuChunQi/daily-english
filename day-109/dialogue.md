🗣 每日对话 Day 109 — 接手导师第一课：新人不该只看文档，得看问题

周四 | 社交生活（第 16 周第三幕：QBR 收官后的剧情新章——Leo 从"被带"变"带人"，正好接上 10 月 2 日试点前的人手缺口；前奏埋 10/15 用户大会钩子）

─────────────────────────
🎬【场景引入】
─────────────────────────

周四 12:20，食堂外。你（Leo）刚吃完午饭，迎面碰上 Maya——刚入职两周的新同事。她就是 Day 104 你在复盘邮件里提的"容量评审常驻人手"：VP 批了两周试点，Maya 就在 10 月 2 日前加入你的项目。

但她的入职体验跟你当年一模一样（Day 99）：面对一座部落知识大山，文档过期，没人有空讲。她今天的问题听起来特别无害："我下一步该看什么文档？"

─────────────────────────
📖【完整对话】
─────────────────────────

**A (Maya, New Hire):** Leo! Do you have a minute? I've been meaning to ask you something.
（Leo！有空一分钟吗？我一直想问你点事儿。）

**B (Leo, Data Platform):** For you? Always. What's on your mind?
（对你来说？随时都有。想问什么？）

**A (Maya):** I've read through all the onboarding docs — twice. They say "check the wiki for details," but the wiki says "ask the data platform team." So… which docs should I actually be reading next?
（我把所有入职文档读了——两遍。文档说"详情见 wiki"，wiki 又说"请咨询数据平台团队"。所以……我下一步到底该读哪些文档？）

**B (Leo):** Ha — welcome to the club. I onboarded onto this system in June. Day one, I was drinking from a firehose. Docs got me maybe 30% of the way there. The other 70% lives in people's heads — we call it tribal knowledge.
（哈——欢迎入伙。我今年六月接手这套系统。第一天就是从消防水管里喝水。文档最多帮我走到 30%。剩下 70% 都活在人的脑子里——我们管它叫部落知识。）

**A (Maya):** That's what I was afraid of. I don't want to bug people with questions the docs should answer.
（我就怕这个。我不想拿那些文档本该回答的问题去烦人。）

**B (Leo):** Look — nobody here expects a two-week-old hire to know this stuff. That's not on you. What matters is how you ask. Three things: what you already tried, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Bring those three, and no one will call it bugging.
（这么说吧——这里没人指望一个入职两周的人懂这些。那不是你的问题。重要的是你怎么问。三样东西：你已经试过什么、你预期发生什么、实际发生了什么。带上这三样，没人会说这是打扰。）

**A (Maya):** Actually — that already paid off. Last week I fixed a broken dashboard filter by comparing it with the staging one. Took me a whole afternoon, but I found it.
（其实——这招已经见效了。上周我靠对比 staging 环境的那个，修好了一个坏掉的看板筛选器。花了一整个下午，但我找到了。）

**B (Leo):** That was you? That fix was solid — it's been running in prod for a week, zero tickets since. You're further along than you think. Here's my honest advice: spend less time reading, more time pulling things apart. The system doesn't break if you poke around in staging.
（那是你修的？那个修复很扎实——在 production 上跑了一周，之后零工单。你比自己以为的走得远。给你句实话：少花时间读文档，多花时间拆东西。你在 staging 里戳来戳去，系统不会坏。）

**A (Maya):** That's honestly a relief to hear. Can I ask you one more thing — the big one?
（听你这么说真是松了口气。我能再问一个吗——那个最大的问题？）

**B (Leo):** Go for it.
（问吧。）

**A (Maya):** You learned this whole system from the person who left. If you could pass on just one thing — the single most useful thing you picked up — what would it be?
（你这套系统是从离职那位同事那儿学的。如果你只能传下去一样东西——你学到的最有用的一样——会是什么？）

**B (Leo):** Write things down as you learn them. When I took over, my safety net was notes nobody else could read — just for me. Six weeks later, that's the onboarding guide new hires actually use. The docs I inherited were outdated the day they were written. The notes weren't — because I wrote them while things were fresh. Pass it on when it's your turn.
（学到什么就随手记下来。我接手的时候，我的安全网是一套只有我自己看得懂的笔记——纯自用。六周后，那就是新员工真正在用的入职指南。我接手的那些文档从写完那天起就过期了。笔记没有——因为我是趁热写的。轮到你的时候，把它传下去。）

**A (Maya):** Deal. I'll open a doc tonight called "Notes nobody asked for" and see where it goes.
（成交。我今晚就开一个文档，叫"没人要的笔记"，看看能写成什么样。）

**B (Leo):** That's the spirit. And Maya — same time next week? I want to walk you through the queue architecture before the October 15th user conference prep kicks off.
（就是这股劲儿。还有 Maya——下周同一时间？我想在 10 月 15 日用户大会的准备工作启动之前，带你过一遍队列架构。）

🎯【语块精讲】（8+1）
─────────────────────────

• **What's on your mind?** — 你在想什么？/想聊什么？（比 "What do you want?" 友好得多的开场问句，信号：我关心的是你这个人，不只是你的问题）
  工作场景：You pinged me at 7 AM — what's on your mind?
  日常场景：You've been quiet all dinner. What's on your mind?

• **drink from a firehose** — 从消防水管里喝水（信息量远超承受力的学习体验；Day 99 学过，今天你亲口用来带新人）
  工作场景：Onboarding week here is drinking from a firehose — pace yourself.
  日常场景：First day in Tokyo: ten neighborhoods, one map app. Total firehose.

• **tribal knowledge** — 部落知识（只存在于老员工脑子里、没写下来的系统知识；本次对话核心）
  工作场景：Half our release process is tribal knowledge — one resignation away from chaos.
  日常场景：Every family has tribal knowledge — like which light switch actually controls the garage.

• **That's not on you.** — 那不是你的问题/责任不在你（把自责从人身上卸下来，钉到系统/环境上）
  工作场景：The docs being outdated isn't on you — that's our backlog.
  日常场景：You missed the exit because the sign was hidden — that's not on you.

• **bug (someone)** — 打扰/烦（某人）（比 bother 口语化；常见否定："I don't want to bug you"=轻量级礼貌求救）
  工作场景：Sorry to bug you — got two minutes to sanity-check my rollback plan?
  日常场景：I hate bugging the neighbors, but could you grab my packages this week?

• **pay off** — 见效/有回报（努力开始产生看得见的收益；D1: pay off 大放异彩，注意对话里现在时的用法）
  工作场景：Investing in those integration tests paid off this quarter — zero rollbacks.
  日常场景：Six weeks of piano practice finally paid off — I played the whole piece without stopping.

• **poke around** — 到处戳戳看看（无目的地探索、动手翻看；Day 58 学过，今天换成 Leo 的建议者视角）
  工作场景：The best way to learn the admin console is to poke around in the staging copy.
  日常场景：We poked around the flea market for an hour and left with a lamp we didn't need.

• **pull (things) apart** — 拆开研究（把东西拆解开来搞懂内部原理；和 poke around 成对：一个动手戳、一个动手拆）
  工作场景：I spent Friday pulling apart our slowest query — found a missing index.
  日常场景：He pulled apart the coffee machine once. It worked again. Nobody knows how.

• 🎁 **彩蛋：pass it on** — 把它传下去（pay it forward 的动词版：把别人给过你的帮助，原样传给下一个需要的人）
  工作场景：Someone showed me the ropes when I joined — now it's my turn to pass it on.
  日常场景：My grandma taught me this dumpling fold — now I'm passing it on to you.

✅【Day 108 变造挑战参考答案】
─────────────────────────

**题 1：公司宣布收购一家初创，团队群里传言四起，你要发一条 Slack 稳住局面。**（get ahead of / the rumor mill）
> "Team — quick facts before the rumor mill gets ahead of us: the acquisition is real, announced 20 minutes ago. What we know: our roadmap is unchanged this year. What we don't know yet: org details — I'll share them here the moment I have them. If you hear something that worries you, flag it here first, not after."

**题 2：向新客户做首次方案介绍，搭档问你想怎么开场。**（lead with）
> "Let's lead with the numbers — their sector, their conversion rate, our result for a similar client. If the first slide shows we've already done our homework on them, the next 20 slides sell themselves."

**题 3：朋友抱怨家庭聚餐总变成对他的"审问"，你教他一招。**（a formality, not a defense 的逻辑）
> "Start texting the family group chat before visits — job update, one photo, one sentence. In two months, the dinner questions are all answered already. The visit becomes a formality, not an interrogation — they ask nothing because they already know everything."

🔄【变造挑战】（3 题）
─────────────────────────

用今天学到的表达，换场景重新说：

1. 新实习生说不敢在 code review 里提问，怕显得基础差。你要在 Slack 里给他打气。
   💡 提示：用 "That's not on you" 和 "bug" 的否定式——把"怕打扰"重新框定为"问得对"

2. 教练朋友向你抱怨：会员总说"健身房没效果"就退卡。你想劝他别自责。
   💡 提示：用 "pay off" 的时间维度——不是没效果，是还没到见效的时间

3. 朋友家的老菜谱只有他奶奶会做，没人写下来。奶奶今年 88 了。
   💡 提示：用 "tribal knowledge" 和 "pass it on" ——这题几乎是送分题，但试着说得更动人

💡【今日一句】
─────────────────────────

**"Write things down as you learn them — your notes are someone's future onboarding guide."**
（学到什么就随手记下来——你的笔记，就是未来某个人的入职指南。）

Maya 今晚就会开一个叫 "Notes nobody asked for" 的文档。三个月后，新来的实习生会问她："这份指南是你写的吗？"——这就是 pass it on 的全部意义。你今天学的每个语块、记的每条笔记，都同时在为两个版本的自己服务：现在的你和接你班的那个。
