今天不推新对话，来一次轻松但超实用的"职场黑话词典"！

很多职场缩写在邮件、Slack、会议里高频出现，
认识它们 = 看懂 80% 的英文工作沟通。

### 📧 邮件 & Slack 缩写 TOP 15

| 缩写 | 全称 | 中文 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| **ASAP** | As Soon As Possible | 尽快 | "Can you review this ASAP?" |
| **EOD** | End of Day | 今天下班前 | "I'll send the report by EOD." |
| **COB** | Close of Business | 营业日结束（≈5pm） | "Please submit by COB Friday." |
| **OOO** | Out of Office | 不在办公室（休假） | "I'll be OOO next Monday." |
| **PTO** | Paid Time Off | 带薪假 | "She's on PTO this week." |
| **FYI** | For Your Information | 供参考 | "FYI, the meeting moved to 3pm." |
| **BTW** | By The Way | 顺便说 | "BTW, great presentation today." |
| **TL;DR** | Too Long; Didn't Read | 太长不看/摘要 | "TL;DR: We need to ship by Friday." |
| **NRN** | No Reply Needed | 不用回复 | "Just an FYI, NRN." |
| **TBD** | To Be Determined | 待定 | "The venue is still TBD." |
| **TBA** | To Be Announced | 待公布 | "Speaker list TBA." |
| **ETA** | Estimated Time of Arrival/Completion | 预计完成时间 | "What's the ETA on the fix?" |
| **AFK** | Away From Keyboard | 暂离（不在电脑前） | "BRB, AFK for lunch." |
| **BRB** | Be Right Back | 马上回来 | "BRB, grabbing coffee." |
| **IMO / IMHO** | In My Opinion / In My Humble Opinion | 在我看来 | "IMO, we should delay the launch." |

### 🗣 职场黑话 TOP 15

这些不是缩写，但每天都会听到：

| 黑话 | 中文 | 例句 | 对应的"正常"说法 |
|------|------|------|------|
| **circle back** | 回头再讨论 | "Let's circle back on this next week." | "Let's discuss this later." |
| **touch base** | 简单沟通一下 | "Can we touch base after lunch?" | "Can we talk briefly?" |
| **ping me** | 发消息给我 | "Ping me if you need anything." | "Message me." |
| **sync** | 同步/对齐 | "Let's sync on the project status." | "Let's get aligned." |
| **align** | 对齐/达成一致 | "We need to align with the design team." | "We need to agree." |
| **take offline** | 会后再说（不在会上讨论） | "Let's take this offline." | "Let's discuss after the meeting." |
| **action item** | 待办事项 | "Any action items from this meeting?" | "What do we need to do?" |
| **deep dive** | 深入探讨 | "We need a deep dive into the data." | "Let's look at this in detail." |
| **low-hanging fruit** | 容易实现的目标 | "Let's start with low-hanging fruit." | "Let's do the easy ones first." |
| **bandwidth** | 精力/时间/资源 | "I don't have the bandwidth this week." | "I'm too busy." |
| **leverage** | 利用 | "We should leverage existing tools." | "We should use." |
| **deliverables** | 交付物 | "What are the deliverables for Q3?" | "What do we need to produce?" |
| **roll out** | 推出/上线 | "We plan to roll out the feature next month." | "We plan to launch." |
| **on the same page** | 达成共识 | "Let's make sure we're on the same page." | "Let's make sure we agree." |
| **move the needle** | 产生显著效果 | "This won't move the needle." | "This won't make a big difference." |

### 💬 场景示范：一天里的缩写串烧

想象你在公司的一天，看看这些缩写怎么自然出现：

**早上 9:00 — 打开 Slack**
> **Tom:** Morning! FYI, I'll be OOO tomorrow — taking PTO. 🏖️
> （早上！供参考，我明天不在办公室——休带薪假。）
>
> **Sarah:** No worries. BTW, the client wants the proposal ASAP. ETA?
> （没问题。顺便说，客户想要提案，越快越好。预计什么时候能完成？）
>
> **Tom:** I'll have it by EOD today.
> （今天下班前给你。）

**上午 10:30 — 项目会议**
> **Manager:** Let's do a quick deep dive into Q3 deliverables. Any blockers?
> （我们来快速深入看看Q3交付物。有什么阻碍吗？）
>
> **Tom:** I don't have bandwidth for the full analysis. Can we start with low-hanging fruit first?
> （我没精力做完整分析。我们能先从容易的开始吗？）
>
> **Manager:** Fair enough. Let's circle back on the rest next week. Action items — Tom, you own the proposal. Sarah, sync with design.
> （有道理。剩下的下周再讨论。待办事项——Tom，你负责提案。Sarah，和设计团队对齐一下。）

**下午 2:00 — Slack 跟进**
> **Sarah:** @Tom gentle reminder — the proposal? 📝
> （Tom，友情提醒——提案呢？）
>
> **Tom:** AFK for lunch, BRB in 20. Almost done, will ping you when ready.
> （午休暂离，20分钟回。快做完了，好了给你发消息。）

**下午 5:30 — 快下班**
> **Tom:** @Sarah proposal is in your inbox. NRN, just review when you can.
> （Sarah，提案在你邮箱了。不用回复，有空看看就行。）
>
> **Sarah:** TL;DR version?
> （给个摘要版？）
>
> **Tom:** TL;DR — 3 options, I recommend Option B. Happy to touch base tomorrow AM.
> （太长不看版——3个选项，我推荐B。明天上午简单沟通一下。）

### 🎯【今日挑战】

**Test Yourself!** 看看你今天学了多少：

1. 同事发消息 "I'll be OOO next week on PTO. Please contact Lisa for anything urgent. NRN." — 这句话什么意思？

2. 你的老板说 "We need to circle back on this and do a deep dive. Let's take it offline." — 他想表达什么？

3. 你要催同事交东西，下面哪个更礼貌？
   - a) I need this ASAP.
   - b) Gentle reminder — what's the ETA on this?

<details>
<summary>📖 参考答案</summary>

1. "我下周休假不在办公室。有紧急事务请联系Lisa。此邮件不用回复。"

2. "我们需要回头深入讨论这个问题，但不在现在这个会议上说，单独聊。"

3. b 更礼貌。"Gentle reminder + ETA" 比 "ASAP" 柔和得多。
   ASAP 听起来像命令，适合紧急情况，日常慎用。

</details>

💡 **今日一句：**
> **"Ping me when you have bandwidth."**
> （你有空的时候发消息给我。）

这句话把两个职场黑话自然组合在一起，实用指数 100%。
建议今天在 Slack 里找机会用一次 `ping me` 或 `bandwidth`！
