## 场景 1：向上级汇报紧急延期风险

**背景：** 你是项目负责人 Alex，项目原定下周五上线，但测试发现了一个严重的性能问题，可能需要延期一周。你需要向产品 VP 做紧急汇报。

**你需要用到这些语块：**
- in a nutshell
- the bottom line is
- on track to → ❌（恰恰相反，要表达"可能不能 on track 了"）
- go up the chain
- don't let perfect be the enemy of good

.

.

.

**参考答案：**

> **Alex:** Thanks for making time, Dana. **In a nutshell**, we found a performance issue in load testing — the system can't handle 10x traffic yet. **The bottom line is**, we're no longer **on track to** launch next Friday. I wanted to **flag this** early so we can decide together: do we ship with known limitations, or push the date by one week?
>
> **Dana:** What's your recommendation?
>
> **Alex:** I'd say **don't let perfect be the enemy of good**. The core features work fine under normal traffic. We can launch with a **skeleton crew** monitoring capacity, and optimize the high-traffic scenario in the first patch. But I wanted to **go up the chain** before making the call.
>
> **Dana:** I appreciate the heads-up. Let's go with your recommendation. Send me a one-pager I can forward to leadership — **put it in plain English**, no jargon.

**用到语块：** in a nutshell / the bottom line is / on track to / flag / don't let perfect be the enemy of good / a skeleton crew / go up the chain / put it in plain English ✅（8个！）

## 场景 2：跨团队站会上发现两个团队在同一个问题上各自为战

**背景：** 你是技术经理 Wei，主持每周跨团队站会。你发现团队 A 和团队 B 各自独立在解决同一个缓存问题，浪费了两周时间。你需要指出问题、统一方向、安排后续讨论。

**你需要用到这些语块：**
- you've got the floor
- can I jump in
- good catch
- go down a rabbit hole
- take this offline
- before we go further down this path

.

.

.

**参考答案：**

> **Wei:** Alright, let's start. Team A, **you've got the floor** — what's your update?
>
> **Team A Lead:** We spent last week optimizing the Redis cache layer. Response times are now 40% faster.
>
> **Wei:** Good. Team B?
>
> **Team B Lead:** We also worked on cache optimization, but from the application side. We built a local in-memory layer that reduces Redis calls by 60%.
>
> **Wei:** **Can I jump in** here? I'm seeing significant overlap between the two approaches. Team A optimized Redis directly, Team B bypassed it with a local cache. **Good catch** on both fronts, but we've essentially spent two weeks solving the same problem from two angles.
>
> **Team A Lead:** Oh — we didn't know Team B was working on this too.
>
> **Wei:** That's exactly why we need to align. **Before we go further down this path**, I want both teams to compare notes and pick one direction. Let's not **go down a rabbit hole** debating which is better in this meeting — **take this offline**, sync tomorrow, and come back with a unified plan.
>
> **Team B Lead:** Agreed. We'll set up a 30-minute call for tomorrow.

**用到语块：** you've got the floor / can I jump in / good catch / before we go further down this path / go down a rabbit hole / take this offline ✅（6个）

## 场景 3：绩效考核 1-on-1：和老板讨论下一季度的成长目标

**背景：** 你是开发工程师 Liang，每季度和工程总监 Rachel 做 1-on-1。这个季度你想提两个目标：1）改善公开演讲能力 2）参与更多架构设计。但你有点紧张，不太敢直接说。

**你需要用到这些语块：**
- set the tone
- get one's act together
- string together
- hold someone to something
- the first thing to go (out the window)

.

.

.

**参考答案：**

> **Rachel:** So Liang, what do you want to focus on next quarter?
>
> **Liang:** Two things. First, I want to get better at speaking up in meetings. I've noticed that when I'm unprepared, I stay quiet — and then my ideas **don't get heard**. I want to **set the tone** by speaking up early in discussions, even if it's just a quick comment.
>
> **Rachel:** I love that. What's the second one?
>
> **Liang:** I want to get more involved in architecture decisions. Last quarter, whenever things got busy, deep design work was always **the first thing to go out the window** — I'd just fix bugs instead of thinking about the bigger picture. I need to **get my act together** and carve out dedicated time for architecture work.
>
> **Rachel:** That's very self-aware. How will you measure progress?
>
> **Liang:** For public speaking, my goal is to **string together** at least one substantive comment in every team meeting for the whole quarter. Not just "I agree" — actual contributions.
>
> **Rachel:** I like that metric. And for architecture?
>
> **Liang:** I'd like to lead one architecture review session per month. Four sessions total.
>
> **Rachel:** Excellent. I'm going to **hold you to that** — I'll check in at our monthly 1-on-1s. And I'll make sure you're invited to the architecture review meetings starting next week.

**用到语块：** set the tone / the first thing to go (out the window) / get one's act together / string together / hold someone to something ✅（5个）
