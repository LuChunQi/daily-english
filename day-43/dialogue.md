🗣 每日对话 Day 43 — 英文邮件里那些"看着像英语但其实不是"的表达
周日 | 轻松英文：职场 Chinglish 翻车现场

─────────────────────────
📖【今日主题】
─────────────────────────

写英文邮件的时候，很多表达我们觉得理所当然——结果外国同事读完一脸问号。

今天我们来盘点那些 **Chinglish 重灾区**：看着像英语，但 native speaker 根本不这么说。

学会用 **地道的替代表达**，你的邮件立刻专业 10 倍。

─────────────────────────
🎯【十大 Chinglish 翻车现场】
─────────────────────────

### 1. ❌ "Please find the attached file."
✅ **"I've attached the file." / "The file is attached."**

> 💡 "Please find the attached" 是 1950 年代的信件用语，现代邮件直接说 "I've attached..." 就好。

**场景对比：**
- Chinglish: Please find the attached file for your reference. 😬
- Native: I've attached the Q3 report — let me know if you have questions. ✅

---

### 2. ❌ "I think we should discuss about this."
✅ **"We should discuss this." / "Let's discuss this."**

> 💡 "Discuss" 是及物动词，**不需要 "about"**。这是最高频的语法错误之一。

**场景对比：**
- Chinglish: Let's discuss about the timeline. 😬
- Native: Let's discuss the timeline in tomorrow's standup. ✅

---

### 3. ❌ "I will revert to you soon."
✅ **"I'll get back to you soon." / "I'll follow up shortly."**

> 💡 "Revert" 在现代英语里 = "恢复到原状"，不是 "回复"。印度英语里常用 revert 代替 reply，但英美职场不这么用。

**场景对比：**
- Chinglish: Please revert back to me by Friday. 😬
- Native: Could you get back to me by Friday? ✅

---

### 4. ❌ "Could you please check and confirm?"
✅ **"Could you confirm this?" / "Can you take a look?"**

> 💡 "Check and confirm" 是典型的中式"核实确认"直译，英语里 "confirm" 已经包含了"核实后确认"的意思。

**场景对比：**
- Chinglish: Please check and confirm the details. 😬
- Native: Could you confirm the details in the attached doc? ✅

---

### 5. ❌ "As per our conversation..."
✅ **"As we discussed..." / "Following up on our chat..."**

> 💡 "As per" 是过时的法律文书用语，日常邮件里显得太正式、太僵硬。

**场景对比：**
- Chinglish: As per our conversation yesterday, I am sending the document. 😬
- Native: As we discussed, here's the document. ✅

---

### 6. ❌ "I am writing to inform you that..."
✅ **直接说事情："Just a heads-up..." / "Wanted to let you know..."**

> 💡 英语邮件讲究开门见山。不需要先宣告"我要告诉你一件事"，直接说就行。

**场景对比：**
- Chinglish: I am writing to inform you that the meeting has been rescheduled. 😬
- Native: Quick heads-up: the meeting's moved to 3 PM. ✅

---

### 7. ❌ "Do the needful."
✅ **"Could you handle this?" / "Can you take care of this?"**

> 💡 这是印度英语的经典表达，英美职场完全不使用。"Do the needful" = "做必要的事"，但听起来又模糊又傲慢。

**场景对比：**
- Chinglish: Please do the needful and revert at the earliest. 😬 (双重翻车)
- Native: Could you handle this when you get a chance? ✅

---

### 8. ❌ "I have some doubts about this plan."
✅ **"I have some concerns about this plan." / "I'm not sure about this approach."**

> 💡 "Doubt" = 不信任/质疑某人能力，语气比 "concern" 重得多。工作里你想表达的是"担忧"，不是"怀疑人品"。

**场景对比：**
- Chinglish: I have some doubts about the timeline. 😬 (听起来像你不信任项目经理)
- Native: I have some concerns about the timeline — can we walk through it? ✅

---

### 9. ❌ "The same is attached herewith."
✅ **"I've attached it." / "It's attached."**

> 💡 "Herewith" 是 19 世纪的法律文书用语。2026 年了，别用了。

**场景对比：**
- Chinglish: Kindly find the same attached herewith. 😬 (三个翻车点：kindly + the same + herewith)
- Native: I've attached it. ✅

---

### 10. ❌ "I will do it today itself."
✅ **"I'll get it done today." / "I'll have it by EOD."**

> 💡 "Today itself" 是"今天就做"的直译，英语里直接说 "today" 就够了，不需要 "itself" 强调。

**场景对比：**
- Chinglish: I will complete it today itself. 😬
- Native: I'll get it done by EOD today. ✅

─────────────────────────
📋【一页速查表：Chinglish → Native】
─────────────────────────

| ❌ Chinglish | ✅ Native |
|---|---|
| Please find the attached | I've attached... / It's attached |
| discuss about | discuss (不加 about) |
| revert to you | get back to you |
| check and confirm | confirm |
| As per our conversation | As we discussed |
| I am writing to inform you | Just a heads-up / Wanted to let you know |
| Do the needful | Handle this / Take care of this |
| I have doubts | I have concerns |
| attached herewith | It's attached |
| today itself | today / by EOD |
| kindly | please (或者直接省略) |
| at the earliest | ASAP / as soon as you can |
| pre-pone | move earlier / reschedule earlier |
| updation | update (名词) |
| intimated | notified / let know |

🔄【变造挑战】（3 题）
─────────────────────────

把下面的 Chinglish 邮件片段改成地道表达：

**1.** "Kindly find the attached document and do the needful at the earliest."
   💡 提示：去掉 kindly + the attached 改写 + do the needful 改写 + at the earliest 改写

**2.** "As per our discussion yesterday, I have some doubts about the timeline. Let's discuss about this in the meeting."
   💡 提示：三个翻车点，逐一替换

**3.** "I am writing to inform you that the release has been preponed. I will revert with details today itself."
   💡 提示：四个翻车点，逐一替换

---

### 参考答案

**1.**
> I've attached the document — could you take a look when you get a chance?

**2.**
> Following up on our chat yesterday — I have some concerns about the timeline. Let's discuss it in the meeting.

**3.**
> Quick heads-up: the release has been moved earlier. I'll follow up with details today.

─────────────────────────
💡 今日一句
─────────────────────────

> **"Less is more — the best email reads like a conversation, not a legal document."**

邮件写得好的人，不是用词高级，而是 **简单清楚**。今天给同事发邮件时，检查一遍：有没有用 "kindly"、"herewith"、"revert"、"do the needful"？有的话，删掉，换成大白话。
