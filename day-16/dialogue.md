🗣 每日对话 Day 16 — 面试中描述项目经历
周一 | 第3周主题：英文面试

─────────────────────────
📖【完整对话】
─────────────────────────

A (Lisa, Interviewer): So, can you walk me through a project you're particularly proud of?
（那么，能跟我讲一个你特别引以为豪的项目吗？）

B (Kevin, Candidate): Absolutely. Last year I led the migration of our entire payment system from a legacy monolith to microservices. It was a six-month project involving three teams.
（当然。去年我主导了把我们整个支付系统从老旧单体架构迁移到微服务。这是一个为期六个月的项目，涉及三个团队。）

A: That sounds like a major initiative. What was your specific role?
（听起来是个很大的项目。你具体负责什么？）

B: I was the tech lead, so I spearheaded the architecture design and set up the migration roadmap. I also contributed to the actual codebase — I personally rewrote the checkout module.
（我是技术负责人，所以我牵头做了架构设计，并制定了迁移路线图。我也参与了实际代码编写——我个人重写了结账模块。）

A: What was the biggest challenge you faced during the project?
（项目期间你面临的最大挑战是什么？）

B: Data consistency was a huge concern. We had to make sure no transactions were lost during the switch. I proposed a dual-write strategy where both systems ran in parallel for two weeks.
（数据一致性是个大问题。我们必须确保切换过程中没有交易丢失。我提出了一个双写策略，两个系统并行运行了两周。）

A: And how did it turn out? Any measurable results?
（结果怎么样？有什么可量化的成果吗？）

B: Yes — we reduced checkout latency by 40% and the system has had zero downtime since the migration. The project was delivered on time and actually came in under budget.
（有的——结账延迟降低了40%，迁移以来系统零宕机。项目按时交付，而且实际花费低于预算。）

A: That's impressive. How did you handle disagreements within the team during the project?
（很了不起。项目期间团队内部有分歧时你是怎么处理的？）

B: There was a debate about whether to use gRPC or REST for internal communication. I encouraged everyone to voice their concerns, then we did a quick proof of concept for both. The data made the decision for us.
（关于内部通信是用 gRPC 还是 REST 有过争论。我鼓励每个人都发表意见，然后我们对两种方案都做了快速的概念验证。数据帮我们做了决定。）

A: Great approach. One last question — what would you do differently if you could do it over?
（很好的方法。最后一个问题——如果能重来一次，你会有什么不同的做法？）

B: I'd invest more time in documentation early on. We were moving fast and some design decisions weren't well captured, which caused confusion later. Lesson learned.
（我会在早期花更多时间写文档。我们当时进展很快，一些设计决策没有很好地记录下来，后来造成了混乱。教训深刻。）

─────────────────────────
🎯【语块精讲】（8 个）
─────────────────────────

• walk someone through — 逐步讲解、引导某人了解
  工作场景：Let me walk you through the quarterly report.（让我带你过一遍季度报告。）
  日常场景：Can you walk me through how this app works?（你能给我演示一下这个 app 怎么用吗？）

• led — 主导、领导（lead 的过去式，面试高频词）
  工作场景：I led a team of five engineers on this project.（我在这个项目中带领一个五人工程师团队。）
  日常场景：She led the hiking group up the mountain.（她带领徒步小组上山。）

• spearheaded — 牵头、发起（比 led 更有力，强调创新性）
  工作场景：He spearheaded the company's sustainability initiative.（他发起了公司的可持续发展项目。）
  日常场景：Our department spearheaded the charity drive this year.（我们部门今年牵头了慈善活动。）

• contributed to — 对…做出贡献、参与了
  工作场景：I contributed to the open-source project in my spare time.（我业余时间给那个开源项目做了贡献。）
  日常场景：Everyone contributed to the success of the event.（每个人都为活动的成功做出了贡献。）

• dual-write strategy — 双写策略（技术面试加分表达）
  工作场景：We used a dual-write strategy to ensure data consistency during migration.（迁移过程中我们用双写策略保证数据一致性。）
  日常场景：I kept two journals — one digital, one paper — as a dual-write approach to not forgetting anything.（我保持两本日记——一本电子的，一本纸质的——双写策略确保不忘事。）

• came in under budget — 实际花费低于预算（面试亮点句）
  工作场景：The renovation project came in under budget by 15%.（装修项目实际花费比预算低15%。）
  日常场景：Our road trip came in under budget because we camped instead of staying in hotels.（我们的自驾游比预算省了钱，因为我们露营没住酒店。）

• proof of concept (PoC) — 概念验证（用小实验证明方案可行）
  工作场景：Before committing to the full build, we did a proof of concept.（全面投入之前我们做了一个概念验证。）
  日常场景：I made a small proof of concept cake before baking the wedding cake.（做婚礼蛋糕之前我先烤了个小试验蛋糕。）

• lesson learned — 学到了教训（面试收尾金句，展示反思能力）
  工作场景：The deployment failure was painful, but lesson learned — always test in staging first.（部署失败很痛苦，但教训深刻——一定要先在预发布环境测试。）
  日常场景：I forgot to bring sunscreen to the beach. Lesson learned — always check the weather.（去海滩忘了带防晒霜。教训——永远先查天气。）

🔄【变造挑战】（3 题）
─────────────────────────
用今天学到的表达，换场景重新说：

1. 你在面试，面试官问"Tell me about a time you solved a difficult problem"。用 spearheaded 和 proof of concept 造一个回答。
   💡 提示：先说问题是什么 → 你怎么牵头 → 做了什么验证 → 结果

2. 你的同事问你一个复杂流程怎么走，用 walk through 和 contribute to 给他解释，并强调团队协作。
   💡 提示：Let me walk you through... / Everyone contributed to...

3. 你在年终总结里要写一个项目成果，用 came in under budget 和 lesson learned 造两句话。
   💡 提示：The project... came in under budget... / Lesson learned...

💡 今日一句：
**"I spearheaded the architecture design and contributed to the actual codebase."**
→ 面试中展示你既能做战略规划又能 hands-on 的万能句型。今天找机会把"I spearheaded... and contributed to..."说出口，哪怕是对自己说。
