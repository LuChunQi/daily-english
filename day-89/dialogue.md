### 🎬 场景 1：代班主持客户 Demo 周会（工作 · 主持）

**背景：** 你的老板 Mia 出差，你第一次主持与客户的 biweekly Demo 会议。Priya 是设计师，Daniel 是客户方技术负责人。

---

**A (你 · 代班主持):** Thanks everyone for joining. Mia's traveling this week, so I'll be running today's sync. Two items: the search redesign demo, then open questions. Priya, kick us off — where do we stand on the redesign?
> 谢谢大家参加。Mia 这周出差，今天的例会由我来主持。两项议程：搜索改版 Demo，然后自由提问。Priya，你先来——改版现在进展如何？

**B (Priya · Designer):** The short version is: all three screens are done and wired up to real data. The one sticking point is the mobile filter panel — it works, but it's rough around the edges.
> 长话短说：三个页面全部完成并接上了真实数据。唯一的卡点是移动端筛选面板——能用，但边角还比较糙。

**A:** Got it. Let's not go down that rabbit hole on styling today — Daniel, does the overall flow work for your team?
> 明白。样式的事咱们今天别陷进去——Daniel，整体流程你们团队用起来没问题吗？

**C (Daniel · 客户方):** The flow's solid. One question though — can users export results to Excel? My analysts live in spreadsheets.
> 流程没问题。不过有个问题——用户能把结果导出到 Excel 吗？我的分析师们离了表格活不了。

**A:** Good question, but that's a roadmap decision — let's park it, and I'll address it in the follow-up email so we keep this call focused.
> 好问题，但这是排期层面的决策——先记到停车场，我在后续邮件里专门答复，今天的通话保持聚焦。

**C:** Fair enough.
> 合理。

**A:** Quick time check — five minutes left. To recap: Priya owns the mobile polish, I own the export question with an answer by Thursday, and Daniel owns collecting analyst feedback. Did I get that right?
> 报个时间——还剩五分钟。总结一下：Priya 负责移动端打磨，我负责导出问题、周四前给答复，Daniel 负责收集分析师反馈。我理解得对吗？

**C:** That's right. Nice job stepping in as host, by the way — smooth meeting.
> 没错。顺便说一句，代班主持得不错——会开得很顺。

**A:** Appreciate it. Notes and action items go out right after this call. We're adjourned — thanks, everyone!
> 谢谢。纪要和行动项会后马上发出。散会——谢谢大家！

**📝 本场景使用语块（10个）：**
run today's sync / kick us off / where we stand on / the short version is / sticking point / go down that rabbit hole / park it / time check / own / We're adjourned（bonus）

### 🎬 场景 2：电梯里被 VP 拦下问迁移进度（工作 · 即兴汇报）

**背景：** 周五下午，你在电梯里遇到 Engineering VP Elena。她刚开完董事会，董事们问了平台迁移的进度。

---

**B (Elena · VP of Engineering):** Ben! Glad I ran into you — the board was asking about the platform migration today. I've been keeping tabs on the dashboards, but numbers only tell half the story. Are we going to make September?
> Ben！正好碰上你——今天董事会问起平台迁移的事。我一直有盯着仪表盘，但数字只能说明一半。我们赶得上九月吗？

**A (你):** The short version is: yes, with one asterisk. We're eighty percent done, and what's left is mostly testing.
> 长话短说：赶得上，但有一个星号。完成了八成，剩下的主要是测试。

**B:** What's the asterisk?
> 什么星号？

**A:** Performance testing. If we hit issues there, fixing them could eat into our two-week buffer.
> 性能测试。如果在那里出问题，修复可能会蚕食我们两周的缓冲。

**B:** And the team? I know this quarter's been brutal.
> 团队状态呢？我知道这个季度很熬人。

**A:** Tired but focused. Nothing's on fire — and if that changes, you'll hear it from me first.
> 累但专注。没有着火——真有情况，你会第一个从我这里听到。

**B:** That's what I needed to hear. Keep me posted — a one-liner after Tuesday's test run is plenty.
> 这正是我需要听的。随时同步——周二测试跑完，给我一句话简报就够了。

**A:** Will do. Don't let me hold you up — aren't you headed to the airport?
> 没问题。不耽误你了——你不是要赶飞机吗？

**B:** (laughs) I am. It's been that kind of quarter. See you Monday, Ben.
> （笑）是啊。这个季度就是这种节奏。周一见，Ben。

**📝 本场景使用语块（10个）：**
glad I ran into you / keep tabs on / the short version is / eat into / nothing's on fire / keep me posted / a one-liner / don't let me hold you up / that kind of week（bonus，此处活用为 that kind of quarter）

### 🎬 场景 3：周六城市农场志愿活动 + 顺路美食节（社交 · 应约）

**背景：** 周四下班前，同事 Mia 兴冲冲来问你周六去不去城市农场做志愿者。

---

**A (Mia · 同事):** Hey! A few of us are volunteering at the city farm this Saturday — planting trees, clearing trails, free lunch included. You in?
> 嘿！我们几个周六要去城市农场做志愿者——种树、清步道，还包午餐。你来吗？

**B (你):** Volunteering… outside… on my day off? (laughs) What's the selling point?
> 做志愿者……在户外……在我休息日？（笑）卖点是什么？

**A:** Fair question! It's only four hours, the farm's forty minutes out, and there's a food festival right next door — so the afternoon is wide open for eating your way through it.
> 问得好！只干四个小时，农场离这儿四十分钟，旁边正好有个美食节——下午时间完全自由，可以从头吃到尾。

**B:** And the work itself? I was hoping not to be roughing it with a shovel for four hours straight.
> 那活儿本身呢？我可不想连着四小时挥铲子吃苦。

**A:** No, no — they split us into small groups. If you're so inclined, you can run the seedling table instead, which is basically standing around labeling plants.
> 不不——他们会分小组。你要是愿意，可以去管幼苗桌，基本就是站着给植物贴标签。

**B:** Honestly, that sounds perfect. Last time I tried gardening, the neighbors' weeds drowned out everything I planted.
> 说实话，听起来太合适了。上次我种点东西，邻居家的杂草把我种的全盖死了。

**A:** (laughs) You'll fit right in. And the farm's sunflowers are showing off this month — great photos for the family group chat.
> （笑）你太适合了。而且这个月农场的向日葵开得跟显摆似的——拍下来发家人群正合适。

**B:** Sold. Count me in — but fair warning, I'm useless before my first coffee, so don't expect much chatter on the drive.
> 被说服了。算我一个——不过丑话说在前，第一杯咖啡下肚之前我基本是废人，车程上别指望我聊天。

**A:** Deal. We leave at eight — I'll send the address tonight.
> 成交。八点出发——今晚我把地址发你。

**📝 本场景使用语块（10个）：**
You in? / what's the selling point / wide open / rough it / if you're so inclined / drown out / show off / Count me in / fair warning（bonus）
