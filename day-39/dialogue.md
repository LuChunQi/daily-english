🎯【语块精讲】（8 个）
─────────────────────────

• **quick check-in** — 快速跟进/问候一下
  工作场景：Hey, quick check-in on the design ticket — is it still on track for Friday?
  日常场景：Just a quick check-in — did you ever book that flight?

• **juggling a couple of fires** — 同时在处理几个紧急问题
  工作场景：Sorry I'm slow to respond, I've been juggling a couple of fires this morning.
  日常场景：Can't talk right now, juggling a couple of fires at home — kid's sick and the plumber's here.

• **slip** —（截止日期）推迟、滑后
  工作场景：The release date slipped from Wednesday to Friday due to some last-minute bugs.
  日常场景：Our dinner reservation slipped from 7 to 8 because the earlier table ran late.

• **blocked on** — 被……卡住了，依赖某物才能继续
  工作场景：I'm blocked on the design review — can't move forward until Sarah signs off.
  日常场景：I'm blocked on the insurance paperwork before I can pick up the car.

• **eat into** — 消耗/侵蚀（时间、预算、资源）
  工作场景：These endless meetings are eating into my coding time.
  日常场景：Rent keeps eating into my travel budget every month.

• **critical path** — 关键路径（决定项目整体进度的环节）
  工作场景：If the API isn't ready, it's on the critical path — everything downstream gets delayed.
  日常场景：Getting the visa is the critical path for our entire trip plan.

• **a lifesaver** — 救命稻草、帮了大忙的人/事
  工作场景：That stub endpoint is a lifesaver — I can finally move forward.
  日常场景：Can you grab the kids from school? You're a lifesaver!

• **we're golden** — 搞定了、没问题了、一切就绪
  工作场景：Once the config is updated, we're golden — deploy whenever you're ready.
  日常场景：I've got snacks, drinks, and the playlist sorted — we're golden for tonight.

🔄【变造挑战】（3 题）
─────────────────────────
用今天学到的表达，换场景重新说：

1. 你在等同事的设计稿才能开始写前端页面，已经等了三天了。发一条 Slack 消息跟进，既要表达紧迫感，又不能显得在催。
   💡 提示：用 quick check-in 开头 + blocked on + eat into

2. 你的项目本来计划本周上线，但因为一个依赖的外部服务还没接好，可能要延期。在站会上跟团队同步这个情况。
   💡 提示：用 slip + critical path + juggling fires 描述原因

3. 同事临时帮你顶了一个紧急 bug，你想在 Slack 上表示感谢。
   💡 提示：用 lifesaver + we're golden + no rush / thanks for jumping on it
