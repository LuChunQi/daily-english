### 场景 1：跨部门项目进度同步会

A (Lisa - Product Manager): Morning everyone. Let's do a quick status check on the Q3 launch. Tom, how's the backend work coming along?
（大家早上好。我们来快速过一下 Q3 发布的状态。Tom，后端工作进展如何？）

B (Tom - Backend Engineer): We're **on track** for the most part. The API integration is **wrapped up**, but we're **blocked on** the third-party payment gateway — they haven't sent us the API keys yet.
（大部分进度正常。API 集成已经完成了，但我们被第三方支付网关卡住了——他们还没给我们 API 密钥。）

A: That's a **good catch**. Have you found a **work around** in the meantime?
（发现得好。你们有找到临时的变通方案吗？）

B: Yeah, we're using a mock server for testing. But we'll need the real keys before we can go live.
（是的，我们目前用模拟服务器做测试。但上线前还是需要真正的密钥。）

A: OK, I'll **loop in** the vendor team and **follow up** on this. Let's **circle back** on Thursday.
（好，我把供应商团队拉进来跟进这件事。我们周四再回头讨论。）

B: Sounds good. I'll send you a **heads up** once I hear back from them.
（好的。一有消息我就提前通知你。）

---

### 场景 2：Slack 上处理紧急 Bug

A (Kevin - QA): 🚨 **FYI** — we found a critical bug in the checkout flow. Orders are failing intermittently.
（🚨 供参考——我们在结账流程里发现了一个严重 bug。订单间歇性失败。）

B (Amy - Frontend Lead): Yikes. Can you **drill down** on which step it's failing at?
（天哪。你能深入看一下是在哪个步骤失败的吗？）

A: It's happening at the payment confirmation step. I've attached the error logs in the thread.
（是在支付确认那一步。我已经把错误日志贴在 thread 里了。）

B: **Good call** on attaching the logs. I'll **chime in** on the incident channel. Should we set up a quick **war room** call?
（贴日志做得好。我会在事件频道里发言。我们要不要开个紧急会议？）

A: Yeah, let me **loop in** David from the backend team too. He knows that module best.
（好的，我把后端的 David 也拉进来。他最熟悉那个模块。）

B: Perfect. **Bumping this** thread so everyone sees it. Let's **take this offline** — I'll set up a call in 5 minutes.
（完美。我顶一下这个帖子让大家都看到。我们线上聊不如开会聊——我5分钟后建个会议。）

---

### 场景 3：周一早上和外国同事聊天

A (Mike - Designer): Hey! **How was your weekend?**
（嘿！周末过得怎么样？）

B (Jenny - Data Analyst): It was great! A few of us went to that new escape room downtown. Have you tried it?
（很棒！我们几个人去了市中心新开的密室逃脱。你试过吗？）

A: No, not yet! **How did it turn out?**
（还没呢！你们结果怎么样？）

B: We actually escaped! Only with 2 minutes left though. It was **close**.
（我们真的逃出来了！虽然只剩2分钟，好险。）

A: Nice! I'm **down** to try it sometime. I heard they have a horror theme room.
（厉害！我很愿意去试试。听说他们有个恐怖主题房间。）

B: Oh yeah, **spoiler alert** — it's terrifying. My friend literally screamed the whole time.
（哦对，剧透警告——超恐怖的。我朋友全程都在尖叫。）

A: Haha, now I definitely have to try it. Hey, since you're the new person on the team, want me to **show you the ropes** around the office this week?
（哈哈，那我更得试了。对了，既然你是团队新人，这周要不要我带你熟悉一下办公室？）

B: That would be awesome! I still get lost trying to find the conference rooms.
（太好了！我现在找会议室还经常迷路。）

✅【参考答案要点】
─────────────────────────

这三个场景覆盖了本周全部 24 个语块的核心用法：

**场景 1 重点复习：** on track, wrapped up, blocked on, good catch, work around, loop in, follow up, circle back, heads up
**场景 2 重点复习：** FYI, drill down, good call, chime in, loop in, Bumping this, take this offline
**场景 3 重点复习：** How was your weekend, How did it turn out, I'm down, spoiler alert, show someone the ropes

─────────────────────────
💡 今日一句
─────────────────────────

> "Let's circle back on this." 

本周出场率最高的表达之一。开会时不知道怎么结束一个话题？这句话完美收尾——"我们回头再讨论"。

建议今天在开会或聊天时，找机会用一次 **circle back** 或 **loop someone in**。这两个是最高频的职场口头禅！

📝 变造挑战参考（Day 01-04 答案汇总）
─────────────────────────

### Day 01 变造挑战
1. 你比计划提前完成了任务 → "I'm **ahead of plan** on this task."
2. 你被另一个项目临时拉走了 → "I got **pulled into** another project."
3. 你需要催某人给反馈 → "Can you get back to me by **EOD**?"

### Day 02 变造挑战
1. 开会时你想补充一点 → "Can I **chime in** here?"
2. 你发现了一个潜在问题 → "I want to **flag** something — I think there's a risk we haven't considered."
3. 会后想继续讨论 → "Let's **take this offline** and **follow up** tomorrow."

### Day 03 变造挑战
1. 提前通知团队明天有维护 → "Just a **heads up** — there's scheduled maintenance tomorrow at 10 AM."
2. 临时方案不够，需要正式解决 → "The **work around** is fine for now, but we need a permanent fix."
3. 在 Slack 上催人回复 → "**Bumping this** — any updates on the design review?"

### Day 04 变造挑战
1. 你也想参加某个活动 → "**I'm down!** Count me in."
2. 别人做的东西快散架了 → "The cake literally **fell apart** when I tried to cut it."
3. 你主动带新人 → "Don't worry, I'll **show you the ropes** — it took me a while to figure things out too."
