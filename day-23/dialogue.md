🗣 每日对话 Day 23 — 项目需求变更：和 PM 讨论范围蔓延
周一 | 第4周主题：深入周会与面试

─────────────────────────
📖【完整对话】
─────────────────────────

场景：Sprint 进行到一半，PM Sarah 突然找到开发 Tom，想加一个新需求。Tom 需要在不伤和气的前提下，把 scope 和优先级谈清楚。

A (Sarah, PM): Hey Tom, got a minute? The client just sent over some feedback — they want to add a bulk export feature to the dashboard.
嘿 Tom，有空吗？客户刚发了反馈，想给 dashboard 加个批量导出功能。

B (Tom, Developer): Sure, I can take a look. But we're already mid-sprint — how does this fit with our current commitments?
可以，我看看。不过我们这周 sprint 已经过半了——这个和我们当前的任务怎么协调？

A (Sarah, PM): I know it's tight. But the client's pushing hard on this one. They're saying it's a deal-breaker for the next renewal.
我知道时间紧。但客户催得很急，说这个是下次续约的硬性条件。

B (Tom, Developer): Understood. In that case, we'd need to deprioritize something else. Right now I'm working on the search optimization — should I put that on hold?
明白。那我们需要把其他任务的优先级降下来。我现在在做搜索优化——要先暂停那个吗？

A (Sarah, PM): Hmm, the search optimization is important too. Could we scope the export feature down to a simpler version for now?
嗯，搜索优化也很重要。我们能不能先把导出功能做精简版？

B (Tom, Developer): That could work. A basic CSV export might take two days instead of five. We can ship first and iterate later.
这个可以。基础的 CSV 导出大概两天，不是五天。我们先出一个版本，后面再迭代。

A (Sarah, PM): Perfect. Let's go with that. Can you write up a brief estimate so I can communicate it to the client?
完美，就这么定。你能写个简要的估时吗？我好跟客户沟通。

B (Tom, Developer): Yeah, I'll send you something by end of day. And let's flag this as a scope change in the sprint review so everyone's aware.
好，今天下班前给你。另外我们在 sprint review 里把这个标为 scope 变更，让大家都知道。

A (Sarah, PM): Good call. I'll update the backlog and make sure the stakeholders are in the loop. Thanks for being flexible on this.
好主意。我会更新 backlog，确保 stakeholders 都知情。谢谢你灵活配合。

🎯【语块精讲】（8 个）
─────────────────────────

• **got a minute?** — 有空吗？/能耽误你一下吗？
  工作场景：Hey, got a minute? I need your input on the deployment plan.
  日常场景：Got a minute? I want to show you something funny.

• **mid-sprint** — sprint 进行到一半（敏捷开发术语）
  工作场景：We're mid-sprint, so pulling in new work means dropping something.
  日常场景：I'm mid-project right now, can we talk next week?

• **deal-breaker** — 硬性条件 / 不能让步的点（谈不成就此吹）
  工作场景：Remote work flexibility is a deal-breaker for me.
  日常场景：Smoking is a deal-breaker for me when it comes to dating.

• **deprioritize** — 降低优先级（不是不做，是往后排）
  工作场景：We need to deprioritize the UI polish and focus on the bug fixes.
  日常场景：I'll deprioritize cleaning the garage and do it next weekend instead.

• **put on hold** — 暂停 / 搁置
  工作场景：The migration project is on hold until we get budget approval.
  日常场景：I had to put my gym membership on hold while recovering from the injury.

• **scope down** — 缩小范围 / 做精简版
  工作场景：Let's scope down the feature to just the core use case for v1.
  日常场景：We scoped down the party to just close friends — too tired for a big gathering.

• **ship first and iterate later** — 先出再说，后面迭代（MVP 思维）
  工作场景：The design isn't perfect, but let's ship it and iterate based on user feedback.
  日常场景：The draft isn't great, but I'll send it now and refine it later.

• **in the loop** — 知情 / 在信息圈内
  工作场景：Please keep me in the loop on any changes to the timeline.
  日常场景：I'm not in the loop on their wedding plans — they haven't told me anything.

🔄【变造挑战】（3 题）
─────────────────────────

用今天学到的表达，换场景重新说：

1. 客户突然要求提前交付，但你的团队已经满负荷了。用 deprioritize 和 put on hold 来回应。
   💡 提示：先表示理解 urgency，然后提出 trade-off——要加急就得降别的优先级。

2. 同事问你能不能帮忙看个 bug，但你正在赶 deadline。用 got a minute? 的回应方式和 scope down 来协商。
   💡 提示：先说现在忙，但可以花 15 分钟快速看一眼（scope down 到最小帮助）。

3. 老板想知道为什么项目延期了，你需要解释中途加了新需求。用 deal-breaker 和 in the loop 来组织语言。
   💡 提示：客户的需求是 deal-breaker，当时没有把所有 stakeholder keep in the loop。

💡 今日一句：Could we scope this down and ship a simpler version first? — 这句话在职场中能救你无数次。面对突如其来的需求，不要说"做不了"，而是说"可以做精简版"。

---

⏰ 跟读建议：重点练习 deal-breaker（/ˈdiːlˌbreɪkər/）和 deprioritize（/diːˈpraɪərətaɪz/）的发音，这两个词在职场沟通中出现频率极高。
