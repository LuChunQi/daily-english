🗣 每日对话 Day 60 — Retro 会上推动流程改进
周二 | 会议发言：建设性提出问题并推动行动

─────────────────────────
📖【完整对话】
─────────────────────────

**背景：** 两周 Sprint 结束的 Retrospective 会议。团队最近两个 Sprint 都因为需求变更导致延期，气氛有些紧张。

**A (Maya, Scrum Master):** Alright, let's move to the retro. I want us to be honest but constructive today. What went well, and what didn't?
（好了，我们进入回顾环节。我希望大家今天坦诚但有建设性。哪些做得好，哪些不好？）

**B (David, Senior Dev):** I'll start with something that's been bugging me. We had three mid-sprint scope changes in the last two weeks, and it really derailed the team's focus.
（我先说个一直困扰我的问题。过去两周我们经历了三次 Sprint 中途需求变更，这真的打乱了团队的专注度。）

**A (Maya):** Thanks for bringing that up, David. Can you give us a specific example so we're all on the same page?
（谢谢你提出来，David。能举个具体例子吗？这样大家信息一致。）

**B (David):** Sure. On Wednesday of week one, we were two days into the payment refactor, and product asked us to add a completely new validation flow. We had to context-switch halfway through, and the code we'd already written went cold.
（当然。第一周周三，我们刚做支付重构两天，产品就要求我们加一个全新的校验流程。我们不得不中途切换上下文，已经写好的代码就搁置了。）

**C (Lisa, PM):** I understand the frustration, and I own that one. But I want to push back a little — that validation came from a compliance requirement we couldn't defer. It wasn't arbitrary.
（我理解大家的沮丧，这个我认。但我想稍微反驳一下——那个校验来自合规要求，我们没法推迟。不是随便加的。）

**B (David):** Fair point. I'm not saying the change wasn't necessary. My issue is the process. We found out mid-sprint, not before it. If we'd known at planning, we could have reprioritized instead of doing the half-finish-and-switch dance.
（有道理。我不是说变更没必要。我的问题是流程。我们是在 Sprint 中途才知道，而不是之前。如果我们在计划阶段就知道，可以重新排优先级，而不是跳这种"做一半再换"的舞。）

**A (Maya):** That's a really good distinction, David — it's not about the "what," it's about the "when." Lisa, is there a way we can get compliance requirements flagged earlier?
（这个区分非常好，David——问题不在于"改什么"，而在于"什么时候改"。Lisa，有没有办法让合规需求更早被标记出来？）

**C (Lisa):** Honestly, yes. I've been thinking about this too. I can set up a biweekly sync with the compliance team so we're not getting last-minute surprises. I'll also create a "compliance pipeline" doc that tracks upcoming requirements.
（说实话，可以。我也在想这个问题。我可以每两周跟合规团队开个同步会，这样就不会到最后时刻才收到意外。我还会建一个"合规管线"文档来追踪即将到来的需求。）

**B (David):** That would be huge. And could we also agree on a rule — if something comes in mid-sprint, it goes into the next sprint unless it's a true P0? That way the team isn't constantly context-switching.
（那就太好了。我们能不能也定个规则——如果 Sprint 中途来了新需求，除非是真正的 P0，否则放到下个 Sprint？这样团队就不会频繁切换上下文了。）

**A (Maya):** I like that. Let's make it an action item. Lisa, you'll own the compliance sync. David, can you draft the "mid-sprint change" policy and circulate it by Thursday?
（我同意。我们把它变成一个行动项。Lisa，你负责合规同步会。David，你能起草一个"Sprint中途变更"策略，周四前发给全员吗？）

**B (David):** Yep, I'll have a draft by Thursday EOD.
（没问题，周四下班前我出个草稿。）

**C (Lisa):** And I'll get the first compliance sync on the calendar this week. Thanks for raising this, David — I know it's not easy to bring up process stuff, but this is exactly what retro is for.
（我这周就把第一次合规同步会排上日历。谢谢你提出来，David——我知道提流程问题不容易，但这正是回顾会的意义。）

**A (Maya):** Perfect. Let's move on to the next item — but I want to capture this as our biggest win of the sprint: we identified a systemic issue and we're walking away with two concrete action items. That's how it should work.
（完美。我们进入下一个议题——但我想把这个记为本 Sprint 最大收获：我们识别了一个系统性问题，并且带着两个具体行动项离开。这就对了。）

🎯【语块精讲】（8 个）
─────────────────────────

**1. be honest but constructive — 坦诚但有建设性**
- 工作场景：I want us to be honest but constructive in this retro.（我希望大家在回顾会上坦诚但有建设性。）
- 日常场景：You can be honest but constructive when giving feedback to your kid.（给孩子反馈时可以坦诚但有建设性。）

**2. derail — 打乱、搅乱（原义：出轨）**
- 工作场景：The mid-sprint scope change really derailed the team's focus.（Sprint中途的范围变更真的打乱了团队专注度。）
- 日常场景：One bad habit can derail your whole morning routine.（一个坏习惯能搅乱你整个晨间流程。）

**3. context-switch — 切换上下文（指从一件事跳到另一件事，效率损失大）**
- 工作场景：We had to context-switch halfway through the refactor.（我们不得不在重构中途切换上下文。）
- 日常场景：Switching between writing and checking emails kills your focus.（在写作和查邮件之间切换会摧毁专注力。）

**4. push back (a little) — 稍微反驳/提出不同意见**
- 工作场景：I want to push back a little — that requirement came from compliance.（我想稍微反驳一下——那个需求来自合规。）
- 日常场景：I pushed back when my friend suggested yet another weekend trip.（朋友又提议周末出行时我提了不同意见。）

**5. own that one — 认领/承认是自己的责任**
- 工作场景：I own that one — I should have flagged it earlier.（这个我认——我应该更早标记出来的。）
- 日常场景：I own that mistake, it was my bad.（那个错误我认，是我的锅。）

**6. be on the same page — 信息一致/达成共识**
- 工作场景：Can you give an example so we're all on the same page?（能举个例子让大家信息一致吗？）
- 日常场景：Let's get on the same page before we talk to the landlord.（跟房东谈之前我们先对一下口径。）

**7. circulate — 发给全员传阅/分发**
- 工作场景：Can you draft the policy and circulate it by Thursday?（你能起草策略并在周四前发给全员吗？）
- 日常场景：I'll circulate the potluck sign-up sheet in the group chat.（我会在群里发聚餐报名表。）

**8. systemic issue — 系统性问题（不是个案，而是流程/结构层面的问题）**
- 工作场景：We identified a systemic issue and fixed it with two action items.（我们识别了一个系统性问题，用两个行动项解决了它。）
- 日常场景：Late buses aren't one driver's fault — it's a systemic issue.（公交晚点不是某个司机的错——是系统性问题。）
