## 🎬 场景 1：季度预算削减会议

**背景：** VP of Engineering 召开紧急会议，宣布下季度预算削减 15%。各部门负责人需要协商如何分配剩余预算。你（Engineering Manager）和 Design Lead 需要表态。

**A — You (Engineering Manager)**
**B — Lisa (Design Lead)**
**C — Tom (VP of Engineering)**

---

**C (Tom):** Thanks for hopping on short notice. The leadership team has decided to cut Q3 engineering budget by 15%. I need you two to figure out how to absorb that without killing any critical initiatives. I know it's not easy.
> 谢谢你们临时赶来。管理层决定削减 Q3 工程预算 15%。我需要你们两个想出怎么消化这个削减，同时不能砍掉关键项目。我知道不容易。

**A (You):** Understood. This means we'll need to **make some hard calls** on which projects to prioritize. I want to make sure we **do full justice** to the initiatives we keep rather than spreading ourselves too thin.
> 理解。这意味着我们得在项目优先级上做一些艰难取舍。我想确保保留下来的项目能做好做到位，而不是撒胡椒面。

**B (Lisa):** I agree. If we **split the team** across too many projects, nothing gets done well. My question is — are the design system overhaul and the mobile redesign both non-negotiable?
> 同意。如果我们把团队拆到太多项目上，什么都做不好。我的问题是——设计系统重构和移动端改版都是**没得商量的**吗？

**C (Tom):** The mobile redesign is **non-negotiable** — it's tied to a customer commitment. The design system can be **scoped down to an MVP**. Can you **chime in** on the engineering side?
> 移动端改版**没得商量**——跟客户承诺挂钩。设计系统可以**缩减到 MVP**。工程这边你能**补充**一下吗？

**A (You):** Sure. From engineering's perspective, if we **scope the design system down to an MVP** — just the core components and tokens — we can ship that in 3 weeks and **bolt on** the advanced variants next quarter. That saves about 40% of the original estimate.
> 好。从工程角度，如果我们把设计系统**缩到 MVP**——只做核心组件和 token——3 周能发，下季度再**叠加**高级变体。这能省掉原估算的 40%。

**B (Lisa):** That works for me. And for the mobile redesign, I'd like to **put my foot down** on keeping the user research phase — without it, we're just guessing. Everything else, we can **cross that bridge when we come to it**.
> 可以。至于移动端改版，我想在用户调研阶段**坚持立场**——没有调研就是在猜。其他的事情，我们**到时候再说**。

**C (Tom):** Fair enough. Send me a revised plan by Friday. And Lisa — I appreciate you not trying to **half-bake** this. Quality over speed on the mobile piece.
> 合理。周五前给我修订后的计划。Lisa——我感谢你没有想**敷衍了事**。移动端这块，质量优先于速度。

---

## 🎬 场景 2：数据团队对口径 — 统一指标定义

**背景：** 市场部和工程部对"活跃用户"的定义不一致，导致季度报告里两个部门的数字差了 20%。你（Data Lead）和市场总监需要在对齐口径之前，先在 Slack 上沟通。

**A — You (Data Lead)**
**B — Megan (Marketing Director)**

---

**A (You):** Hey Megan, I'm **heads-down** on the Q2 report but wanted to flag something before it becomes a bigger issue. Our active user numbers don't match — engineering shows 1.2M, marketing shows 960K. Can you **spare** 15 minutes to **sanity-check** our definitions?
> 嘿 Megan，我正**埋头**做 Q2 报告，但想在事情变大之前先提一下。我们的活跃用户数字对不上——工程显示 120 万，市场显示 96 万。你能**抽** 15 分钟来**快速核对**一下定义吗？

**B (Megan):** Good catch. I was **pulled into** a budget meeting that **ran long**, just saw your message. I'll be honest, I'm **a bit skeptical about** engineering's numbers — last quarter they were higher too. **How confident are you in** the dedup logic?
> 好发现。我之前被**拉去**一个**拖很长**的预算会，刚看到消息。说实话，我对工程的数字**有点怀疑**——上个季度他们也是偏高。你对去重逻辑**有多大把握**？

**A (You):** Let me **address that head-on**. We deduplicate by device ID + user ID hash. After **backing that out** to match your definition (unique users by email), we get 980K — which is much closer to your 960K. The gap is mostly explained by test accounts.
> 让我**正面回应**。我们按设备 ID + 用户 ID 哈希去重。**扣除**差异后按你们的定义（按邮箱去重），我们得到 98 万——跟你们的 96 万接近多了。差距主要是测试账号。

**B (Megan):** Okay, that's more believable. The original 1.2M **feels like a stretch**. But going forward, we really need **a single source of truth** — I don't want to explain a 20% discrepancy to the board again.
> 好吧，这合理多了。原来的 120 万确实**感觉夸张**了。但以后我们真的需要一个**唯一权威数据源**——我不想再向董事会解释 20% 的差异。

**A (You):** Completely agree. I'm **not trying to nitpick**, but if we align on one definition now, we'll be **on solid ground** for all future reporting. The **blast radius** of inconsistent numbers is huge — it affects forecasts, headcount, everything.
> 完全同意。我**不是在挑刺**，但如果现在统一一个定义，以后所有报告都**建立在可靠基础上**。数字不一致的**影响范围**太大了——影响预测、招聘，所有事。

**B (Megan):** Well said. Let's doc this up and circulate to both teams. And thanks for catching this early — **better safe than sorry** when it comes to board-facing numbers.
> 说得好。我们把定义文档化发给两个团队。谢谢及早发现这个问题——面向董事会的数字，**宁可小心也别出事**。

**A (You):** Exactly. I'll send a proposal by EOD. **I owe you one** for being so quick to align.
> 没错。今天下班前我发个方案。这么快就对齐了，**欠你一个人情**。

## 🎬 场景 3：外国同事带你体验密室逃脱

**背景：** 周末，美国同事 Ryan 邀请你（Chen）去玩密室逃脱（Escape Room）。你从没玩过，Ryan 给你讲解规则，你们在游戏中配合默契。

**A — Ryan (American colleague)**
**B — You (Chen)**

---

**A (Ryan):** Hey! Ready for your first escape room? Don't worry, I'll **walk you through** everything. The basic idea is we're locked in a room and have to solve puzzles to get out within 60 minutes.
> 嘿！准备好迎接你的第一次密室逃脱了吗？别担心，我会**一步步教你**。基本概念是我们被锁在一个房间里，要在 60 分钟内解谜逃出来。

**B (You):** Sounds fun! I've heard of these but never tried one. Beyond the name, I honestly have no idea what to expect.
> 听起来很好玩！听说过但从没试过。除了名字，说实话我完全不知道会是什么样的。

**A (Ryan):** That's totally fine — you'll be fine, you're **a quick study**. The key is to communicate: if you find a clue, shout it out. Oh, and **keep your eyes on** the small details — numbers on walls, patterns on the floor. Everything could be a clue.
> 完全没问题——你会做得好的，你**学得快**。关键是沟通：发现线索就喊出来。还有，**盯紧**小细节——墙上的数字、地板上的图案。任何东西都可能是线索。

**B (You):** Got it. Oh wait, this painting has a number on the back — "7391". Could that be a combination?
> 明白。等等，这幅画背面有个数字——"7391"。可能是密码吗？

**A (Ryan):** Nice find! Let's try it on that lock over there… Yes! It opened! You **hit it out of the park** on your first try!
> 找得好！试试那边那把锁……开了！你第一次就**做得极其出色**！

**B (You):** Ha! I think I'm getting **hyped** about this. The adrenaline is real. Back in China, my friends would **get hyped over** escape rooms too — they're getting really popular there.
> 哈！我觉得我开始**兴奋**了。肾上腺素是真的。在国内，我朋友们也为密室逃脱**疯狂**——现在那边也越来越火了。

**A (Ryan):** The whole room just **went wild** when that lock clicked open — well, it was just us two, but still! Hey, there's a note in this drawer. It says "When in Rome…"
> 刚才锁打开的一瞬间整个房间都**沸腾**了——好吧就咱俩，但也是！嘿，这个抽屉里有张纸条。上面写着 "When in Rome……"

**B (You):** "…do as the Romans do!" That's a classic phrase. Maybe it means we need to look at the Roman numerals on the wall? When in Rome, right?
> "……入乡随俗！"经典句子。也许意思是要看墙上的罗马数字？**入乡随俗**嘛！

**A (Ryan):** Brilliant! That must be the next puzzle. See, I told you you'd pick this up fast. Now let's decode those Roman numerals before time runs out.
> 太聪明了！那肯定是下一个谜题。看吧，我说了你上手很快。来，在时间到之前把那些罗马数字解了。
