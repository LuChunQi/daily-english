🗣 每日对话 Day 107 — QBR 现场：三页幻灯片、一个 Ask

周二 | 会议发言（第 16 周新篇章第一幕：Day 99-106 剧情线已收官，今天 QBR 正式开场——Leo 上台 15 分钟）

─────────────────────────
🎬【场景引入】
─────────────────────────

周二 10:05，Quarterly Business Review。你（Leo）是数据平台负责人，今天有 15 分钟。你只有三页幻灯片：周五发生了什么、修了什么、需要一个什么决策。

台下坐着 VP of Engineering Maggie Chen——以"问题尖锐、决策果断"著称。会前你和 Elena 对好了口径（Day 106）：不甩锅、Growth 主动认领试点。现在轮到你自己上台了。VP 会问三个最难接的问题：有多危险？为什么我现在才知道？你到底要我干什么？

─────────────────────────
📖【完整对话】
─────────────────────────

**A (Leo, Data Platform):** Thanks, Maggie. I'll keep this to three slides — what happened Friday, what we fixed since, and one decision I need from this room today.
（谢谢 Maggie。我只讲三页——周五发生了什么、之后我们修了什么、以及今天需要这间会议室给我的一个决策。）

**B (Maggie, VP of Engineering):** Three slides and one ask — I like that already. Walk me through Friday. And Leo, level with me: how close did we come to losing the QBR dashboards themselves?
（三页幻灯片一个诉求——这个开场我已经喜欢上了。给我讲讲周五。还有 Leo，跟我说实话：我们离"连 QBR 看板本身都丢掉"有多近？）

**A (Leo):** Fair question. The queue hit 92% saturation at 6:40 AM Friday. Another hour at that rate, and today's numbers would've been stale. We caught it with an hour of headroom to spare.
（问得有道理。周五早上 6:40，队列达到 92% 饱和。按那个速度再来一小时，今天的数据就已经过期了。我们截住它时，还剩一小时的余量。）

**B (Maggie):** Then explain this to me — why am I hearing about it in a QBR? I run on a no-surprises rule, Leo.
（那你给我解释一下——为什么我要到 QBR 上才听说这件事？我的原则是不允许意外，Leo。）

**A (Leo):** Nothing was sprung on you — the postmortem went to all stakeholders the same day, and your chief of staff was on the thread. Today is the follow-through: the fix shipped Thursday, and the dashboards have been fresh since 7 AM.
（没有人对您搞突然袭击——复盘报告当天就发给了所有相关方，您的幕僚长也在邮件链上。今天讲的是后续落实：修复周四已上线，看板数据从今早 7 点起就是新的。）

**B (Maggie):** Fine. Now the proposal — ten minutes per launch. Real number, or a guess?
（行。现在说提案——每次发布十分钟。实测数字，还是拍脑袋猜的？）

**A (Leo):** We ran the numbers on the last six launches. Median: eleven minutes. Worst case: twenty-two. That's exactly why the pilot is two weeks — we measure first, then commit to a real SLA instead of a promise.
（我们算了最近六次发布的账。中位数：11 分钟。最坏情况：22 分钟。这正是试点要设两周的原因——先测量，再对一个真正的 SLA 承诺，而不是空口许愿。）

**B (Maggie):** And Growth actually agreed to go first? Teams don't usually volunteer for process.
（然后 Growth 真的同意第一个上了？团队一般可不会主动认领流程类的事。）

**A (Leo):** Elena did — twenty minutes ago, in this room. The slide says "Pilot lead: Elena (volunteered)." Her framing, not mine: growth outpaced visibility, and both teams own the fix.
（Elena 认领了——就在二十分钟前、这间会议室里。幻灯片上写着"试点负责人：Elena（主动请缨）"。这是她的框定，不是我安排的：增长跑赢了可见性，两个团队共同承担修复。）

**B (Maggie):** Volunteered, not assigned — that's how you make a process stick. Alright, what's the ask? Budget? Headcount? My sign-off?
（主动请缨，不是被指派——这才叫让流程真正立得住。行了，你到底要什么？预算？人头？还是我的签字？）

**A (Leo):** None of those. Two weeks of runway and a decision on October 2nd. If the numbers hold, you approve the rollout. If they don't, we kill it — and I'll bring you something better.
（都不是。两周的跑道，加上 10 月 2 日的一个决策。数据成立，您批准全量上线；不成立，我们就砍掉它——我再给您带一个更好的方案来。）

**B (Maggie):** You're not asking me to rubber-stamp this, are you? Because I will hold you to those metrics.
（你不是要我不看内容、走过场签个字吧？我可是会拿这些指标盯你到底的。）

**A (Leo):** Please do. On October 2nd, the data does the talking. Worst case, we spent ten minutes per launch to learn we were wrong — a small price to pay for never repeating Friday.
（请您盯。10 月 2 日，让数据说话。最坏情况，我们花每次发布十分钟，发现自己是错的——比起周五的事故再重演一次，这点代价微不足道。）

**B (Maggie):** Approved. Two weeks, then we review. And Leo — "no surprises" means you tell me while it's a footnote, not when it's a headline.
（批准。两周，然后复盘。还有 Leo——"不允许意外"的意思是：趁事情还是脚注的时候就告诉我，而不是等它变成头条。）

**A (Leo):** Understood. You'll hear it from me first — consider that part of the runway too.
（明白。您会最先从我这里听到——就把这个也算进这两周跑道的一部分吧。）

🎯【语块精讲】（8 + 1）
─────────────────────────

• **What's the ask?** — 你到底要什么？（决策/预算/人头/签字）exec 会议黄金句
  工作场景：Love the analysis — now what's the ask? Budget, people, or just my sign-off?
  日常场景：You've listed ten problems with the apartment. What's the ask — a refund or a new place?

• **level with me** — 跟我说实话、打开天窗说亮话
  工作场景：Skip the polish and level with me — can we ship by Friday or not?
  日常场景：Level with me, doc — is this injury serious or just annoying?

• **spring (something) on someone** — 搞突然袭击、让某人措手不及
  工作场景：Don't spring a scope change on me the night before the release.
  日常场景：They sprang a surprise quiz on us — half the class wasn't even awake.

• **run the numbers** — 算账、做测算（用数据支持判断）
  工作场景：Before we commit, let's run the numbers on server costs for the next quarter.
  日常场景：I ran the numbers — driving is cheaper than flying once you count parking.

• **make it stick** — 让（流程/决定/习惯）真正立得住、执行得下去
  工作场景：A rule announced once in Slack won't stick — build it into the review checklist.
  日常场景：My New Year's gym routine finally stuck once I found a workout buddy.

• **rubber-stamp** — 走过场式批准（不看内容直接盖章）
  工作场景：The board isn't here to rubber-stamp management's plan — they'll challenge it.
  日常场景：The visa officer actually read my documents; it wasn't a rubber-stamp interview.

• **the data does the talking** — 让数据说话（用结果代替争辩）
  工作场景：No need to defend the design — ship the A/B test and let the data do the talking.
  日常场景：Stop arguing about whose route is faster — let the GPS do the talking.

• **a small price to pay** — （换取更大保障的）小代价
  工作场景：Paying for the extra backup cluster is a small price to pay for zero data loss.
  日常场景：Waking up at 5 AM for a sunrise hike? Small price to pay for that view.

• 🎁 **彩蛋：a footnote, not a headline** — 趁事情还是"脚注"时说，别等它变"头条"（坏消息要早报）
  工作场景：I flagged the vendor risk in March — it stayed a footnote instead of becoming a headline.
  日常场景：Tell your landlord about the leak now, while it's still a footnote, not a headline.

✅【Day 106 变造挑战参考答案】
─────────────────────────

**题 1：电梯 5 分钟和老板聊晋升**（make them count / carry more weight）
> "Mike, we've only got this elevator ride, so let me make it count: I've led the payments rebuild end to end this quarter, and I'd like to be on the senior-engineer list this cycle. I know that case carries more weight coming from you in calibration — can I count on your support?"

**题 2：同事担心幻灯片把他写成"延期原因"**（frame it as / walk in on the same page）
> "Relax — we'll walk in there on the same page. I'm not going to frame the delay as your module's fault; I'll frame it as a scope surprise none of us caught. Your slide says 'caught it early, re-planned together.' Nobody's name goes on the problem."

**题 3：新流程试用期的预期管理**（set expectations / get behind）
> "Before this rolls out, let me set expectations: the first two weeks are a voluntary trial — nobody is forced in. If it actually saves us time, we decide together whether to make it permanent. I'd love the team to get behind it, but only after you've seen it work."
