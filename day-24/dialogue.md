周二 | 第4周·深入周会与面试

📖【完整对话】
─────────────────────────

场景：两周一次的跨团队 sync 会议。Alex (Platform 团队 Tech Lead) 和 Jenny (Product 团队 PM) 需要对齐 Q3 合作项目的优先级和交付节奏。

A (Jenny, Product PM): Alright, let's get started. Alex, thanks for making time. I wanted to sync up on the Q3 roadmap and make sure we're aligned on priorities before the leadership review next week.
好吧，我们开始吧。Alex，谢谢你抽时间。我想同步一下 Q3 路线图，确保我们在优先级上对齐，下周要给领导层汇报。

B (Alex, Platform Tech Lead): Yeah, good timing. I was going to reach out actually. We've got a few things in flight, and I want to flag a potential resourcing conflict before it becomes a problem.
好的，时间刚好。其实我正要找你。我们手头有几件事在推进，我想提前提一个资源冲突的问题，免得到时候被动。

A (Jenny, Product PM): That's exactly what I was hoping to discuss. What's the conflict?
这正是我想聊的。什么冲突？

B (Alex, Platform Tech Lead): So the API migration was supposed to wrap up by end of July. But the infra upgrade got pushed back two weeks, which means my team can't start the migration until mid-August. That pushes the whole timeline.
API 迁移原定七月底完成。但基础设施升级推迟了两周，意味着我的团队要到八月中旬才能开始迁移，整个时间线都要往后推。

A (Jenny, Product PM): Okay, thanks for the heads up. How does that impact the user-facing features we committed to for the August release?
好的，感谢提醒。那对我们承诺的八月版本的用户侧功能有什么影响？

B (Alex, Platform Tech Lead): Honestly? The search redesign and the notification overhaul are both blocked on the migration. I'd recommend we deprioritize the notification piece and focus all effort on search. That way at least one major feature ships on time.
说实话？搜索改版和通知系统重构都卡在迁移上。我建议把通知那个降级，集中火力做搜索，这样至少一个主要功能能按时上线。

A (Jenny, Product PM): I hear you, but notifications are a big ask from the sales team. Let me push back a little — can we scope down the notification overhaul instead of cutting it entirely? Maybe just do the top three user triggers for August, and hand off the rest to Q4?
我理解，但通知系统是销售团队强烈要求的。我稍微推回来一下——能不能不砍掉，而是缩减范围？八月只做最重要的三个触发场景，剩下的移交给 Q4？

B (Alex, Platform Tech Lead): That could actually work. If we limit it to three triggers, my team can handle that in parallel with the search work. But I need you to get buy-in from sales — I don't want them coming back mid-sprint asking why their feature got cut in half.
这倒可以。如果只做三个触发场景，我的团队可以和搜索并行推进。但你得让销售那边认可——我不想跑到一半他们又来问为什么功能被砍了一半。

A (Jenny, Product PM): Deal. I'll set up a call with the sales leads tomorrow and loop you in if there are any curveballs. Can we also lock in a bi-weekly sync so this doesn't drift again?
成交。我明天约销售负责人开个会，有问题拉你进来。我们能不能定一个双周同步会，免得又跑偏？

B (Alex, Platform Tech Lead): Sounds good. Let's put it on the calendar — every other Wednesday, 30 minutes. I'll send a summary to both teams so everyone's on the same page.
没问题。放日历上吧——隔周三，半小时。我会给两个团队发个纪要，确保大家都在同一频道。

🎯【语块精讲】（8 个）
─────────────────────────

• sync (up) — 同步信息/对齐进展
  会议场景：Let's sync up on the Q3 roadmap before the review.
  日常场景：Hey, haven't talked in a while — want to grab coffee and sync up?

• aligned (on something) — 在某事上达成一致
  会议场景：I want to make sure we're aligned on priorities before we present to leadership.
  日常场景：We're not aligned on where to go for vacation — I want beaches, she wants mountains.

• in flight — 进行中的（类似 in progress / underway）
  会议场景：We've got three initiatives in flight right now.
  日常场景：I've got a couple of job applications in flight, waiting to hear back.

• flag — 提前提出/标记（潜在问题）
  会议场景：I want to flag a potential resourcing conflict before it blows up.
  日常场景：Just flagging that the restaurant closes at 9, so we should hurry.

• blocked on — 被…卡住/阻塞
  会议场景：The notification feature is blocked on the API migration.
  日常场景：I'm blocked on your approval — can you sign off today?

• scope down — 缩小范围（反义：scope creep — 范围蔓延）
  会议场景：Instead of cutting it entirely, can we scope down to the top three items?
  日常场景：The renovation budget is too high — we need to scope down the kitchen remodel.

• buy-in — 利益相关方的认可/支持
  会议场景：I need you to get buy-in from sales before we change the plan.
  日常场景：If you want to change the team's workflow, you need everyone's buy-in first.

• hand off / handoff — 移交/交接
  会议场景：We'll do the first phase, then hand off the rest to the QA team.
  日常场景：I'm going on PTO Thursday — can you handle the handoff to the client?

🔄【变造挑战】（3 题）
─────────────────────────
用今天学到的表达，换场景重新说：

1. 你不是在会议上，而是在 Slack 上跟同事说：项目卡在等待法务审批，建议先做其他部分。
   💡 提示：用 blocked on + flag + scope down

2. 你在面试中被问到："How do you handle cross-team collaboration?"
   💡 提示：用 sync up + aligned + buy-in 编一个简短回答

3. 你要在邮件里通知全组：原来负责的人离职了，工作要移交给另一个人。
   💡 提示：用 hand off + loop in + on the same page

💡 今日一句：Let's make sure we're aligned before we present to leadership.
→ 今天开会时，试着用 aligned 替代 agree——更专业，也更符合外企开会语境。
