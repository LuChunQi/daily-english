场景一：跨部门项目 kickoff 被临时叫停
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

背景：你是 Tech Lead Alex，产品总监 Mia 突然叫停启动会，说预算还没批。你需要安抚团队、重新对齐、并给出下一步行动。

**A (Mia, Product Director):** Alex, before we **get this show on the road**, I need to flag something. Finance hasn't signed off on the Q3 budget yet. We might need to **put everything on hold**.
> Alex，在我们正式开始之前，我得提一件事。财务还没批准 Q3 预算，我们可能得先暂停。

**B (Alex, Tech Lead):** Understood. Let's not **devolve into** panic here. We can **stage this in phases** — Phase 1 only needs a skeleton crew, so the budget impact is minimal.
> 明白。但别慌，我们可以分阶段来——第一阶段只需要精简人手，预算影响很小。

**A (Mia):** That's a fair point. But I don't want to **pull the trigger** on something half-funded. Can we **sleep on it** and reconvene tomorrow?
> 有道理。但我不想在资金不到位的情况下就拍板。我们能不能放一晚，明天再碰？

**B (Alex):** Works for me. I'll **loop in** Finance in the meantime and get a clearer picture. Better to **over-communicate** at this stage than rush it.
> 没问题。我先把财务拉进来，搞清楚状况。这个阶段宁可多沟通也别赶。

**A (Mia):** Great. And make sure the team knows we're still **in good shape** — this is a procedural delay, not a cancellation.
> 好。另外确保团队知道我们状态没问题——这只是流程上的延迟，不是取消。

**B (Alex):** Will do. Everyone's **aligned** on the roadmap. Once the budget clears, we can **hit the ground running**.
> 收到。大家对路线图认知是一致的，预算一到位就能马上跑起来。

**A (Mia):** Perfect. Thanks for not making me **play devil's advocate** here — I'm actually on your side.
> 完美。谢谢你没让我唱白脸——其实我是站你们这边的。

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
场景二：Slack 上紧急协调：生产环境告警，需要别的团队支援
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

背景：你是 DevOps工程师 Ben，凌晨收到告警，需要后端组 Raj 帮忙排查。在 Slack 上协调。

**A (Ben, DevOps):** @Raj **got a sec**? We've got a P1 alert on the payment service. I need someone who knows the order flow to help me **dig into** this.
> Raj 有空吗？支付服务有 P1 告警，我需要一个懂订单流程的人帮我排查。

**B (Raj, Backend):** Yikes. What's the **blast radius**?
> 不好。影响范围多大？

**A (Ben):** About 15% of transactions are timing out. I've already **looped in** the on-call DBA. She says the **happy path** is fine — it's an edge case with currency conversion.
> 大概 15% 的交易超时。我已经把 DBA 拉进来了，她说正常流程没问题——是货币转换的边缘场景。

**B (Raj):** Got it. Let me **take that on**. Is there a **drop-in replacement** for the conversion module, or are we patching in place?
> 明白，我来跟进。汇率转换模块有可直接替换的方案吗，还是我们就地修？

**A (Ben):** Patching for now. I'll **build in a buffer** on the retry logic so we don't hammer the downstream service.
> 先就地修。我会在重试逻辑里加缓冲，避免把下游打爆。

**B (Raj):** **Fair heads-up** — I'll need about 30 minutes to write and test the fix. Can you hold the fort?
> 提前说一声——我大概需要 30 分钟写完并测试修复。你能先顶着吗？

**A (Ben):** No worries. **Better to over-communicate** — I'll post updates every 10 mins in this thread so everyone stays **in the loop**.
> 没问题。宁可多沟通——我会每 10 分钟在这个 thread 里更新一次，确保所有人都在知情圈内。

**B (Raj):** You're **a lifesaver**. Once this is stable, I'll write up the postmortem.
> 你真是救星。等稳定了我会写事故报告。

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
场景三：公司年会后的聚餐：跟其他部门的同事建立人脉
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

背景：年会上，你是刚升职的工程师 Chris，在自助餐桌边遇到了设计组的 Nora。你们不熟，但都想拓展跨部门人脉。

**A (Nora, Design):** Hey, you're Chris from Platform, right? **Glad you could make it** to the dinner.
> 嘿，你是 Platform 组的 Chris 对吧？你能来聚餐太好了。

**B (Chris, Platform):** Thanks! Yeah, I don't usually come to these things, but I figured it'd be good to **put faces to names** across teams.
> 谢谢！我一般不太参加这种活动，但觉得能跟其他部门的同事混个脸熟挺好的。

**A (Nora):** Totally. I'm still **finding my feet** in the design org — only joined three months ago. It feels like there's a lot to learn about **who owns what**.
> 完全同意。我还在设计组适应中——才来三个月。感觉要搞清楚谁负责什么还挺多的。

**B (Chris):** I hear you. When I started, I basically **hoped for the best** and asked a lot of questions. People here are pretty willing to help if you **pitch in** when they need it.
> 我懂。我刚来的时候基本就是边问边摸索。只要你愿意在别人需要的时候搭把手，这里的人都很乐意帮忙。

**A (Nora):** That's good to hear. Hey, **if you're so inclined**, we have a monthly cross-functional coffee chat. It's super informal — just trading war stories.
> 那太好了。对了，如果你感兴趣的话，我们每月有一次跨部门咖啡聊天，非常非正式——就是互相聊聊天分享经验。

**B (Chris):** I'll **take you up on that**! Is it just design, or are other teams invited too?
> 我接受你的邀请！只是设计组，还是其他团队也参加？

**A (Nora):** All orgs. The more, the merrier. Last month someone from Infra shared a **war story** about a deploy that **devolved into** a full rollback at 2 AM.
> 所有部门。人越多越好。上个月 Infra 组的人分享了一个凌晨两点部署失败全线回滚的惨案。

**B (Chris):** Ha! I remember that incident. We were **rowing in the same direction** that night — everyone chipped in. Anyway, I'll DM you about the next coffee chat.
> 哈哈！我记得那次。那天晚上大家劲往一处使——所有人都出力了。好了，我回头私信你问咖啡聊天的事。
