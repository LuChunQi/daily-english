今天我们把这个**口头汇报**改写成**正式的季度业务回顾邮件**（Quarterly Business Review, QBR），对比口语版和书面版的差异，掌握从"说"到"写"的转换技巧。

选这个场景的原因：季度/月度汇报邮件是每个职场人都会写的高频文档，也是上级评估你"能不能讲清楚价值"的关键窗口。

📄【正式邮件版】
────────────────────────-

**To:** Diana Chen, VP of Engineering
**From:** Alex Liu, Senior Engineer
**Subject:** Q2 Engineering Recap & Q3 Resource Proposal
**Date:** July 4, 2026

---

Diana,

**Q2 Summary**

Q2 was a strong quarter for the infrastructure team. Below is a summary of key outcomes, misses, and a proposal for Q3 resourcing.

**Highlights**

- **Infrastructure migration — shipped 2 weeks ahead of schedule, zero downtime.** We phased the migration into three batches and deprioritized non-essential scope early, which kept the team focused and reduced risk. Three downstream teams were unblocked as a result.

**Areas that fell short**

- **API gateway refactor — delivered, but with significant schedule overrun.** We encountered undocumented dependencies in the legacy codebase, resulting in a 40% timeline extension. In hindsight, we underestimated the technical debt in this domain and did not allocate sufficient buffer time.

**Q3 Proposal: Two-Track Approach**

Given the technical debt exposed in Q2, I recommend a dual-track plan for Q3:

1. **Debt reduction track:** One dedicated sprint to address the API gateway debt, estimated at 2 engineers × 2 weeks.
2. **Feature delivery track:** The remaining team continues on the Q3 roadmap without interruption.

This approach allows us to address the root cause without stalling feature delivery. I've drafted a one-pager with scope, headcount, and timeline estimates (attached).

**Request**

I'd appreciate your review of the attached proposal by **Wednesday, July 9**. If you're comfortable with the direction, I'm happy to join the leadership sync next week to answer any questions.

Thanks,
Alex

**Attachment:** Q3_Gateway_Debt_Proposal_v1.pdf

🔍【口语 vs 书面：逐句对照分析】
────────────────────────-

下面把 Day 37 对话中的关键句子和邮件中的对应表达放在一起对比，看看从"说"到"写"到底变了什么。

### 对照 1：开场

| 口语版 (Day 37) | 书面版 (邮件) |
|---|---|
| "Hi Diana, thanks for making time. I put together a quick recap of Q2 — what went well, what didn't, and where I think we should focus next quarter." | "Q2 was a strong quarter for the infrastructure team. Below is a summary of key outcomes, misses, and a proposal for Q3 resourcing." |

**转换要点：**
- 口语用 "thanks for making time" 破冰 → 书面直接结论先行（BLUF: Bottom Line Up Front）
- 口语 "what went well, what didn't" → 书面 "key outcomes, misses"（名词化）
- 口语 "where I think we should focus" → 书面 "a proposal for Q3 resourcing"（更具体）

---

### 对照 2：说成果

| 口语版 | 书面版 |
|---|---|
| "The big win was the migration project — we shipped it two weeks ahead of schedule with zero downtime. That unblocked three teams who were waiting on the new infrastructure." | "Infrastructure migration — shipped 2 weeks ahead of schedule, zero downtime. We phased the migration into three batches and deprioritized non-essential scope early. Three downstream teams were unblocked as a result." |

**转换要点：**
- 口语 "the big win was" → 书面用 bullet point 标题直接写结果（scannable）
- 口语 "we phased it into three chunks and cut the non-essential pieces up front" → 书面 "phased... into three batches and deprioritized non-essential scope"（cut → deprioritized，更正式）
- 补充了原因解释（"which kept the team focused and reduced risk"），书面邮件需要更多逻辑论证

---

### 对照 3：说失败/不足

| 口语版 | 书面版 |
|---|---|
| "The API gateway refactor took way longer than estimated. We kept uncovering hidden dependencies — every time we fixed one thing, two more would break. I think we underestimated the technical debt in that area." | "API gateway refactor — delivered, but with significant schedule overrun. We encountered undocumented dependencies in the legacy codebase, resulting in a 40% timeline extension. In hindsight, we underestimated the technical debt in this domain and did not allocate sufficient buffer time." |

**转换要点：**
- 口语 "took way longer than estimated" → 书面 "significant schedule overrun"（名词短语，精确化）
- 口语 "every time we fixed one thing, two more would break" → 书面删掉（过于口语化的描述，用 "undocumented dependencies" 一词概括）
- 书面增加了具体数字 "40% timeline extension"（口语不说精确百分比，书面要有数据支撑）
- 书面加了反思："In hindsight... did not allocate sufficient buffer time"（主动承认问题+提出教训）

---

### 对照 4：提方案

| 口语版 | 书面版 |
|---|---|
| "I'd advocate for a two-track approach: one sprint dedicated to paying down the debt in the gateway, while the rest of the team keeps shipping features. I don't think we can afford to keep kicking the can down the road." | "I recommend a dual-track plan for Q3: 1. Debt reduction track: One dedicated sprint... 2. Feature delivery track: ... This approach allows us to address the root cause without stalling feature delivery." |

**转换要点：**
- 口语 "I'd advocate for" → 书面 "I recommend"（更标准的商务用语）
- 口语 "kicking the can down the road"（习语）→ 书面 "address the root cause"（正面表述替代习语）
- 口语线性描述 → 书面用编号列表（numbered list），一目了然
- 增加了人力和时间估算 "2 engineers × 2 weeks"（书面需要 actionable 细节）

---

### 对照 5：行动项

| 口语版 | 书面版 |
|---|---|
| "Will do. I'll have it on your desk by Wednesday." | "I'd appreciate your review of the attached proposal by Wednesday, July 9." |
| (Diana: "Put together a one-pager... and I'll take it to the leadership team next week.") | "If you're comfortable with the direction, I'm happy to join the leadership sync next week to answer any questions." |

**转换要点：**
- 口语 "on your desk by Wednesday" → 书面 "review... by Wednesday, July 9"（具体日期+明确动作）
- 书面主动提出参加领导会议（"happy to join the leadership sync"），把被动等待变成主动推进

✏️【写作技巧讲解：6 个核心转换法则】
─────────────────────────

### 法则 1：BLUF — 结论先行

口语可以铺垫（"thanks for making time" → "let me walk you through..."），邮件不行。

**邮件第一段就要回答三个问题：**
- 这封邮件讲什么？（Q2 回顾 + Q3 提案）
- 结论是什么？（Q2 表现好，但暴露了技术债，Q3 需要双轨方案）
- 我要你做什么？（审阅附件，下周开会讨论）

### 法则 2：名词化 — 把"动词短语"变成"名词短语"

| 口语（动词短语） | 书面（名词短语） |
|---|---|
| we shipped it early | early delivery |
| it took longer than planned | schedule overrun |
| we found hidden dependencies | undocumented dependencies |
| we cut non-essential scope | deprioritization of non-essential scope |

名词化让邮件读起来更紧凑、更专业。但也不要过度——每段 1-2 个就够了。

### 法则 3：用 bullet point 代替段落

口语是线性的（A 说一句，B 回一句），邮件是扫描式的。

**读者习惯：** 先看标题 → 再看 bullet → 最后看行动项。如果正文是一大段，90% 的人只读第一句。

**适用场景：**
- 成果清单（Highlights）
- 问题列表（Areas that fell short）
- 方案步骤（Q3 Proposal）
- 行动项（Action Items）

### 法则 4：习语 → 正式表达

| 口语习语 | 书面替代 |
|---|---|
| boil the ocean | attempt to address everything at once |
| kick the can down the road | defer the problem / postpone resolution |
| ship ahead of schedule | deliver ahead of plan |
| fall short | did not meet the target |
| get behind something | endorse / support |
| come down to | the key factor is |

**原则：** 不是说习语"不好"，而是在邮件（尤其是向上汇报）中，正式表达更稳妥。万一邮件被转发给更高层，习语可能造成理解障碍。

### 法则 5：数字精确化

口语可以模糊（"way longer"、"a lot of dependencies"），邮件要给数字。

| 口语 | 书面 |
|---|---|
| took way longer | 40% timeline extension |
| three teams were waiting | three downstream teams were unblocked |
| one sprint | One dedicated sprint (2 engineers × 2 weeks) |

**为什么重要：** 上级做决策需要数据支撑。"significant delay" 不如 "40% overrun"；"some resources" 不如 "2 engineers × 2 weeks"。

### 法则 6：主动语气 + 明确的行动项

口语中行动项可以是模糊的约定（"I'll have it on your desk by Wednesday"），邮件中的行动项必须精确：

**好：** "I'd appreciate your review of the attached proposal by Wednesday, July 9."
**更好：** "Action items: (1) Diana — review attached proposal by Wed 7/9. (2) Alex — present at leadership sync on Thu 7/10."

**万能模板：**
```
[人名] to [动词] [具体内容] by/on [日期].
```

🛠【实操练习：口语→书面转换】
─────────────────────────

试试把以下口语句子改写成邮件表达：

**练习 1：**
口语："We tried to boil the ocean last time and it was a disaster. This time we broke it into phases and it worked way better."
💡 提示：名词化 + 删习语 + 加因果
<details>
参考答案：The previous attempt to address all requirements simultaneously resulted in significant delays. This time, a phased approach enabled earlier delivery with reduced risk.
</details>

**练习 2：**
口语："Honestly, I think we keep kicking the can down the road on this tech debt. It's eating into our velocity."
💡 提示：习语→正式 + 名词化 + 精确描述影响
<details>
参考答案：Continued deferral of technical debt resolution is impacting development velocity. I recommend allocating dedicated capacity in Q3 to address this.
</details>

**练习 3：**
口语："I can get behind that. Put together a one-pager and I'll take it to the leadership team."
💡 提示：换成第一人称主动 + 明确时间线
<details>
参考答案：I endorse this direction. I'll prepare a one-page summary by Wednesday and present it at the leadership review on Thursday.
</details>

📐【万能模板：季度/月度汇报邮件】

以后写汇报邮件，直接套这个结构：

```
Subject: [Quarter/Month] Recap & [Next Period] Proposal

[人名],

[1句话结论 — BLUF]

[时间段] Summary

[2-3句话概述整体表现]

Highlights
• [成果1] — [量化结果]. [1句原因/影响].
• [成果2] — [量化结果]. [1句原因/影响].

Areas that fell short
• [问题1] — [量化影响]. [1句原因分析].

[Next Period] Proposal

[2-3句话描述方案和理由]

1. [Track/Phase 1]: [具体内容]. [人力×时间].
2. [Track/Phase 2]: [具体内容]. [人力×时间].

Request

[明确要对方做什么，by when]

Thanks,
[你的名字]
```
