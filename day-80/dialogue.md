🗣 每日对话 Day 80 — 1-on-1 谈晋升：跟老板聊职业发展
周一 | 职业发展规划

─────────────────────────
📖【完整对话】
─────────────────────────

**背景：** David (Engineering Manager) 和 Alex (Senior Engineer) 约了半小时 1-on-1，讨论 Alex 下一步的职业发展方向。Alex 想知道从 Senior 到 Staff 需要什么。

**A (David - EM):** So Alex, you mentioned in the agenda you wanted to talk about your career trajectory. Let's dig in — where do you see yourself in the next 12 to 18 months?
（你提议程里说想聊职业发展方向，我们来聊聊——未来12到18个月，你觉得自己想往哪走？）

**B (Alex - Senior Eng):** Honestly, I'd like to make a push for Staff Engineer next review cycle. I feel like I've been operating at that level on the payments project, but I want to understand what the gap is.
（说实话，我想在下一个评审周期冲一下 Staff Engineer。我觉得我在支付项目上已经在那个级别做事了，但我想知道差距在哪。）

**A (David):** Good — I appreciate you being direct about it. Let me be upfront with you: the bar for Staff is genuinely high. It's not just about technical depth, it's about organizational impact. Can you walk me through what you think the expectations are?
（好——我欣赏你这么直接。我也跟你坦诚说：Staff 的标准确实很高。不只是技术深度，还要有组织层面的影响力。你能说说你觉得期望是什么吗？）

**B (Alex):** I think it's about owning a domain end-to-end, driving cross-team initiatives, and mentoring others. I've been doing the first two on payments, but I'll admit the mentoring piece has taken a backseat.
（我觉得是：端到端负责一个领域、推动跨团队项目、带人。前两个我在支付项目上做了，但带人这块确实被我放到了次要位置。）

**A (David):** That's a fair self-assessment. Here's the thing — the promotion committee doesn't just look at what you did, they look at whether someone would struggle without you. Right now, if you left the payments project tomorrow, could the team absorb the loss?
（这个自我评估挺中肯的。问题在于——晋升委员会不只看你做了什么，他们看的是：如果你不在了，团队会不会卡住？现在如果你明天离开支付项目，团队能消化这个损失吗？）

**B (Alex):** Probably not smoothly, to be honest. I'm the only one with full context on the reconciliation logic. That said, I know "being indispensable" isn't the goal — it's a red flag if anything.
（说实话，可能不太顺利。我是唯一对对账逻辑有完整上下文的人。不过我也知道，"不可替代"不应该是目标——如果真是这样的话反而是危险信号。）

**A (David):** Exactly. So here's what I'd like us to do between now and the next cycle: I want you to start delegating the day-to-day on payments and pick up a broader scope. Think architecture-level decisions, not just your component. I'll make sure you're in the room when we do roadmap planning.
（没错。所以这是我想你在下个周期之前做的：开始把支付的日常事务授权出去，承担更广的范围。想架构层面的决策，不只是你那个模块。做路线图规划的时候我会让你参与进来。）

**B (Alex):** That makes sense. Can we put together a concrete plan — like, what does success look like 3 months from now? I don't want this to be one of those conversations that just... evaporates.
（有道理。我们能一起列个具体计划吗——比如，3个月后成功长什么样？我不想这种聊完就……就没有然后了。）

**A (David):** Absolutely. I'll draft something by end of week — a growth plan with specific milestones. And let's check in on it every two weeks during our 1-on-1s so it doesn't fall off the radar.
（当然。这周结束前我起草一个——带具体里程碑的成长计划。我们每两周在 1-on-1 里过一次进度，别让它掉到地上。）

**B (Alex):** Sounds good. And David — thanks for not sugarcoating it. I'd rather know now than be surprised at review time.
（好的。还有 David——谢谢你没跟我打太极。我宁愿现在就知道，而不是评审时才被惊到。）

**A (David):** That's what these 1-on-1s are for. You've got the technical chops — now let's work on the leverage.
（1-on-1 就是干这个的。技术能力你有——现在我们来解决杠杆的问题。）

─────────────────────────
🎯【语块精讲】（8 个）
─────────────────────────

**1. career trajectory — 职业发展轨迹/路径**
  工作场景：Where do you see your career trajectory going in the next few years?（你觉得未来几年职业路径怎么走？）
  日常场景：She changed her career trajectory completely by switching from finance to tech.（她从金融转行到科技，完全改变了职业轨迹。）

**2. make a push for — 冲刺/争取某个目标**
  工作场景：I want to make a push for Staff Engineer in the next review cycle.（我想在下个评审周期冲刺 Staff Engineer。）
  日常场景：If we make a push for it, we can finish the renovation before summer.（如果加把劲，我们能在夏天前完成装修。）

**3. be upfront (with someone) — 坦诚说/开门见山**
  工作场景：Let me be upfront with you — the timeline is aggressive.（跟你坦白说——时间线很紧。）
  日常场景：I should be upfront about the fact that I've never done this before.（我得坦白说，我从没做过这个。）

**4. take a backseat — 让位/退居次要位置**
  工作场景：Mentoring has taken a backseat because I've been heads-down on delivery.（带人的事退居其次了，因为我一直在埋头赶交付。）
  日常场景：My hobby took a backseat when the baby was born.（孩子出生后，我的爱好就先放放了。）

**5. absorb the loss — 消化/承受损失（此处指团队没了某人能不能扛住）**
  工作场景：If the lead engineer quits, can the team absorb the loss?（如果主力工程师离职，团队扛得住吗？）
  日常场景：The company can absorb the loss of one bad quarter.（公司能承受一个季度的亏损。）

**6. fall off the radar — 从视野中消失/被遗忘（聊完就没然后了）**
  工作场景：I don't want this initiative to fall off the radar after today's meeting.（我不想这个项目在今天的会后就被遗忘了。）
  日常场景：We used to talk every day, but after he moved we kind of fell off each other's radar.（我们以前天天聊，但他搬走后就渐渐不联系了。）

**7. sugarcoat — 粉饰/美化（不说实话）**
  工作场景：Don't sugarcoat it — tell me what the real risks are.（别美化——告诉我真正的风险是什么。）
  日常场景：She didn't try to sugarcoat the fact that the restaurant was closing.（她没有试图粉饰餐厅要关门的事实。）

**8. have the chops — 有实力/有能力（口語）**
  工作场景：You've got the technical chops — now you need the leadership skills.（技术实力你有——现在需要领导力。）
  日常场景：Does she have the chops to run a marathon?（她有跑马拉松的实力吗？）

🔄【变造挑战】（3 题）
─────────────────────────
用今天学到的表达，换场景重新说：

**1.** 你不是在聊晋升，而是在跟同事讨论要不要换组。
   💡 用 *career trajectory* 和 *make a push for* 造句。

**2.** 你在告诉一个新来的同事：不要只埋头做事，带人也重要。
   💡 用 *take a backseat* 和 *have the chops* 提醒 ta。

**3.** 你刚跟老板谈完一个新项目提案，老板说要考虑考虑。你担心这事不了了之。
   💡 用 *fall off the radar* 和 *sugarcoat* 表达你的担忧。

💡 今日一句：
**"I don't want this to be one of those conversations that just evaporates."**
→ 每次跟老板聊发展方向，一定要加上一句"跟进机制"，否则聊完真就没了。
