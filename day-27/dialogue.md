### 🎬 场景 1：向上级汇报 Demo 反馈，请求调整优先级

**背景：** 你刚给 VP 做完产品 Demo，他反馈说某个功能很关键，要求提前上线。你需要和团队同步这件事。

**挑战：用至少 6 个本周语块组织你的汇报。**

<details>
<summary>📝 参考答案（点击展开）</summary>

---

**A (You):** Hi everyone, quick **sync up** — I just did the demo for the VP.

**B (Tech Lead):** Oh nice. How did it go?

**A:** Really well overall. I **walked him through** the new dashboard, and **as you can see** from his reaction, the analytics module is now **front and center** for him.

**B:** That's great. Did he have any asks?

**A:** Yeah, he wants us to **bump up** the reporting export feature. He said it's a **deal-breaker** for the Q3 launch.

**B:** But we're **mid-sprint**. We can't just **roll out** something half-baked.

**A:** I know. I suggested we **scope down** the export to CSV only for now. **Ship first and iterate later**. He was okay with that as long as we keep him **in the loop**.

**B:** Alright. Let me **flag** this to the dev team today. We should be able to **hand off** the spec by tomorrow.

**A:** Perfect. That said, we'll need to **deprioritize** the notification redesign. Can you make that call?

**B:** Yeah, I'll put it **on hold**. Getting **buy-in** from the VP on this trade-off was smart.

**A:** He was actually really easy to convince. Once I showed him the timeline, he said **"I'm sold."**

**B:** Ha, love it. Let's **sort out** the details in standup tomorrow.

> **用到语块（14个）：** sync up, walk through, as you can see, front and center, bump up, deal-breaker, mid-sprint, roll out, scope down, ship first and iterate later, in the loop, flag, hand off, that said, deprioritize, on hold, buy-in, I'm sold, sort out

### 🎬 场景 2：Slack 上跟另一个时区的同事协调紧急 Bug 修复

<details>
<summary>📝 参考答案（点击展开）</summary>

---

**A (You) → Slack DM:**

> Hey Raj, **got a sec**? Quick **heads-up** — we're seeing some errors in the payment service. Not sure if it's the **double-wrap** logic we shipped yesterday. Can you take a look before you log off?

**B (Raj):** Sure, **fire away**. What's the error rate?

**A:** About 2% of transactions failing. I know you're about to wrap up your day, so just wanted to **factor that into** your handover notes if we can't **sort it out** now.

**B:** Let me check... yeah, I see it. It's the new **roll out** — the validation layer isn't handling the edge case we discussed. We should **scope down** the validation to unblock payments **as-is**, then fix properly tomorrow.

**A:** Good call. Can you push a hotfix? This is **front and center** for our e-commerce client.

**B:** On it. Should take 15 mins. I'll **loop you in** on the PR.

**A:** Thanks Raj, you're a lifesaver. I know you've been **running on fumes** with **back-to-back** incidents this week.

**B:** Haha, no worries. Just need my **coffee fix** and I'll get it done. This whole situation is a reminder that we need to **blow off steam** this weekend.

**A:** Agreed. Once this is wrapped, let's all take a break. This new monitoring tool has been a **game-changer** though — at least we caught it early.

### 🎬 场景 3：绩效考核后的 1-on-1：讨论新季度目标

**背景：** 你和经理进行季度 1-on-1。他对你上季度的表现满意，想让你在下季度 **lead** 一个新项目。你需要确认资源、范围和期望。

**挑战：用至少 6 个本周语块完成这段对话。**

<details>
<summary>📝 参考答案（点击展开）</summary>

---

**A (Manager):** So, overall great work last quarter. The demo you presented to the board — that really put our team **front and center**. I'd like you to **spearhead** the new API gateway project.

**B (You):** Thanks, I appreciate that. I'd love to **take it away** on this one. Can we **sync up** on scope and resources first?

**A:** Of course. What's on your mind?

**B:** Two things. First, I want to make sure we're **aligned** on what's **in flight** vs what's net-new. The auth refactor is still ongoing — I don't want to **put that on hold**.

**A:** Agreed. Auth stays. We'll **deprioritize** the internal tools revamp instead.

**B:** Perfect. Second thing — I'll need **buy-in** from the infra team. They've been **swamped** lately, so I want to **flag** this early and **loop them in** before we commit to a timeline.

**A:** Smart. Can you draft something and **hand it off** to me by Friday? I'll take it **in front of** the leadership team.

**B:** Will do. One more thing — **that said**, if we're going to **ship first and iterate later**, I want to make sure we're not creating tech debt. Can we agree on a minimum viable scope?

**A:** Absolutely. **Scope down** to the core endpoints, get it out, then iterate. That's exactly the right approach.

**B:** Great. I'll **sort out** the details and send you a summary. Oh, and thanks again — after the crazy **back-to-back** sprints last quarter, I was **running on fumes**. Glad the work paid off.

**A:** It really did. And make sure you take some time to **blow off steam** this weekend. You've earned it.
