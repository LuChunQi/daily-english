# Day 18 变造挑战参考答案

## 1. 酒店前台办理入住

**You:** Hi, I'd like to **check in**. I have a reservation under the name Alex Chen.
（你好，我想办理入住。我预订的名字是 Alex Chen。）

**Receptionist:** Welcome! Could I see your ID? And how many bags do you have?
（欢迎！能出示一下证件吗？您有几件行李？）

**You:** Just one **carry-on** suitcase. **One more thing** — what time is breakfast served?
（就一个登机箱。还有一件事——早餐几点开始？）

**Receptionist:** Breakfast is from 6:30 to 10 AM in the restaurant on the second floor. Enjoy your stay!
（早餐早上 6:30 到 10 点，在二楼餐厅。祝您入住愉快！）

---

## 2. 行李没到，去行李服务台询问

**You:** Excuse me, my **checked bag** didn't arrive. I just **went through** customs after a **layover** in Tokyo.
（打扰一下，我的托运行李没到。我刚在东京转机过了海关。）

**Agent:** I'm sorry about that. Can I see your baggage tag? We'll track it down.
（很抱歉给您带来不便。能看看行李标签吗？我们会追踪到。）

**You:** Sure, here it is. How long does it usually take to locate a missing bag?
（好的，给您。找到丢失的行李一般要多久？）

**Agent:** Usually within 24 hours. We'll deliver it to your hotel once we find it.
（通常 24 小时内。找到后我们会送到您的酒店。）

---

## 3. 问同事出差旅途

**You:** Hey, welcome back! **How was your flight?** Was it easy to **get to downtown** from the airport?
（嘿，欢迎回来！航班怎么样？从机场到市区方便吗？）

**Colleague:** It was pretty smooth actually. I took the BART — got to the hotel in 30 minutes.
（其实挺顺利的。我坐了 BART，30 分钟就到酒店了。）

**You:** Nice. Next time I fly to SFO, I'll do the same. Did you **have a safe flight** overall?
（不错。下次飞旧金山我也这么走。整体旅途还顺利吧？）

**Colleague:** Yeah, no issues at all. The **layover** in Tokyo was a bit tight though.
（嗯，完全没问题。就是东京转机时间有点紧。）
