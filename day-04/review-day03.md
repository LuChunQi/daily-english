# Day 03 变造挑战 — 参考答案

## 1. 你在 Slack 上提醒同事明天有个重要的客户会议，问他准备好了没。

> **参考：** Hey @Tom, quick heads up — we've got the client demo tomorrow at 10 AM. Just wanted to check, are we on track with the prep?

提示词用到了：
- **heads up** — 提醒一下
- **on track** — 进度正常/来得及吗

---

## 2. 你的代码被另一个团队的接口卡住了，你在 Slack 上告诉项目经理这个情况，并说你有替代方案。

> **参考：** Hey Sarah, I'm currently blocked on the payment API — the infra team hasn't provisioned the test environment yet. I can work around it by mocking the API responses for now. Should be unblocked by EOD.

提示词用到了：
- **blocked on** — 被……卡住
- **work around** — 绕过去/替代方案

---

## 3. 你需要在群聊里把新来的实习生拉进一个项目的讨论，同时提醒大家明天下班前交周报。

> **参考：** @channel, I'm looping in @Jessie for the mobile app project — she'll be helping with QA. Also, gentle reminder: please submit your weekly reports by EOD tomorrow (Thursday). Thanks!

提示词用到了：
- **loop (someone) in** — 把某人拉进来
- **EOD** — 今天下班前

---

_这些参考答案只是其中一种写法，换成你自己的场景和语气会更好！_
