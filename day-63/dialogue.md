**📝 场景 1：新人第一次参加架构评审会**

*背景：新来的工程师 Jenny 第一次参加跨团队架构评审，被 Tech Lead 突然问意见。她需要即兴表达，既专业又谦逊。*

A (Tech Lead Rick): Jenny, you've been quiet — you just went through our auth system onboarding last week, right? Any thoughts on the proposed migration plan?
（Jenny，你一直没说话——你上周刚做完我们认证系统的 onboarding，对迁移方案有什么想法？）

B (Jenny, New Engineer): Honestly, I'm still ___①___ about it. The onboarding gave me a good overview, but it was ___②___ — I'm still getting ___③___. That said, from what I've seen so far, I wonder if we might be ___④___ here.
（说实话，我还在___。Onboarding 给了我一个大概，但信息量太大了——我还在熟悉中。不过从我目前看到的，我在想我们是不是有点操之过急了。）

A: Can you ___⑤___ that a bit? Where do you think we're ___⑥___?
（能展开说说吗？你觉得哪里走太快了？）

B: Sure. The current system isn't perfect, but it works. I noticed the auth flow actually ___⑦___ pretty logically once you understand the token lifecycle. I'm worried a big migration might ___⑧___ the team's focus from the features we promised this quarter. And honestly, some of the deployment pain feels like a CI/CD problem ___⑨___ an architecture problem.
（当然。现在的系统不完美，但能用。我注意到认证流程一旦理解了 token 生命周期就顺理成章了。我担心大规模迁移会打乱团队这季度承诺的功能。而且说实话，部署的痛苦感觉像是 CI/CD 的问题伪装成了架构问题。）

A: That's a really ___⑩___ — I hadn't considered that angle. Let's ___⑪___ on this after the meeting.
（观察得不错——我没考虑过这个角度。我们会后单独再聊。）

> **答案：** ① on the fence ② a lot to take in ③ up to speed ④ jumping the gun ⑤ unpack ⑥ getting ahead of ourselves ⑦ falls into place ⑧ derail ⑨ disguised as ⑩ good catch ⑪ circle back

**📝 场景 2：Slack 上协调紧急客户反馈**

*背景：客户反馈某功能在特定条件下崩溃，前端工程师 Sam 需要在 Slack 上异步协调后端和产品，紧急但不慌乱。*

A (Sam, Frontend): @diana @prakash ___①___ — we just got a ticket from a major account. The export feature crashes when the dataset exceeds 10K rows. I've started to ___②___ the logs and I think we're in the ___③___ — it's a memory issue on the backend side.
（提醒——刚收到大客户的工单。导出功能在数据集超过1万行时崩溃。我开始查日志了，我觉得影响范围涉及后端的内存问题。）

B (Diana, Product): How visible is this? Should we ___④___ a warning ___④___ users right away?
（多明显？要不要立刻给用户展示警告？）

A (Sam): Not yet — let's ___⑤___ first. If we show a warning before understanding the ___⑥___, we'll panic people unnecessarily. I'd rather we ___⑦___ — show a friendly retry message instead of a crash.
（先不要——我们先深入排查。在不了解影响范围之前就展示警告会让用户恐慌。我宁可优雅失败——展示一个友好的重试提示而不是崩溃。）

C (Prakash, Backend): Looking at it now. Yeah, it's definitely a memory spike. I can push a hotfix that adds pagination to the export endpoint. Quick question — should I ___⑧___ this myself or wait for code review?
（在看了。确实是内存飙升。我可以推一个热修复给导出接口加分页。快速问一下——我自己处理还是等 code review？）

A (Sam): Good call on the pagination. ___⑨___ — we shouldn't skip review even for hotfixes. But let's expedite it. I'll ping Carlos to review within the hour.
（分页的好主意。我们不应该跳过 review，即使是热修复。但我们加速。我去 ping Carlos 一小时内 review 完。）

B (Diana): Makes sense. ___⑩___ in the ticket — no blame, just facts.
（有道理。工单里语气保持中性——不怪人，只说事实。）

A (Sam): Agreed. Let's ___⑪___ in an hour. If the fix works, I'll draft a post-mortem by EOD.
（同意。一小时后再同步。如果修复有效，我下班前写个事后复盘。）

> **答案：** ① heads-up ② dig into ③ blast radius ④ surface…to ⑤ dig into ⑥ blast radius ⑦ fail gracefully ⑧ own that one ⑨ Be honest but constructive / Good call ⑩ Keep the tone neutral ⑪ circle back

**📝 场景 3：跨团队周会上讨论工具迁移**

*背景：工程效能团队提议全公司迁移到一个新的 CI/CD 平台，各团队代表在周会上讨论。一些人有保留意见但不想泼冷水。*

A (Facilitator): Thanks everyone for joining. The proposal is to migrate all teams to the new CI platform by end of Q3. I want us to ___①___ today — what concerns do people have?
（感谢大家。提议是Q3底前全公司迁移到新 CI 平台。我希望大家今天坦诚但有建设性——有什么顾虑？）

B (Team A Lead): I'll go first. I'm not trying to ___②___ on this initiative — I actually think the new platform is great. But I think we might be ___③___ on the timeline. My team just got ___④___ on the current pipeline, and switching now would ___⑤___ our Q3 deliverables.
（我先说。我不想给大家泼冷水——我其实觉得新平台很好。但我觉得时间线可能有点操之过急。我团队刚在现有流水线上手了，现在换会打乱我们 Q3 的交付。）

C (Team B Lead): I'm ___⑥___ with that. We also just got comfortable with the current setup. The difference between the two platforms isn't exactly ___⑦___ — sure the new one is better, but is it worth the migration cost right now?
（我同意。我们团队也刚适应现有的。两个平台的差距也没到天壤之别——新的是更好，但现在值得迁移成本吗？）

A (Facilitator): ___⑧___ — those are valid concerns. But let me play devil's advocate. If we keep delaying, we'll never make the switch. At some point you just have to ___⑨___ and commit.
（好的——这些都是合理顾虑。让我唱个反调。如果一直推迟，永远也迁不过去。总得有人做出决定。）

B (Team A Lead): That's fair. What if we do a phased rollout? Team C already volunteered as the pilot — if their experience is positive, the rest of us can follow next quarter. That way we're not ___⑩___ more than we can chew.
（有道理。如果分阶段推呢？C 团队已经志愿做试点——如果他们体验好，其他人下季度跟上。这样我们也不会贪多嚼不烂。）

A (Facilitator): I like that approach. Let me ___⑪___ a one-pager with the phased timeline and ___⑫___ it to all team leads by Friday. We can revisit at next week's sync.
（我喜欢这个方案。我整理一份一页纸的分阶段时间线，周五前发给所有团队负责人。下周同步会上再讨论。）

> **答案：** ① be honest but constructive ② throw cold water ③ jumping the gun ④ up to speed ⑤ derail ⑥ on the same page (or: I see where you're coming from) ⑦ night and day ⑧ Fair point / Good catch ⑨ make the call / get your hands dirty ⑩ biting off ⑪ draft / put together ⑫ circulate
