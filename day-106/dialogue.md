周一 9:35，Quarterly Business Review 还有 25 分钟开场。上周五你（Leo）从一场数据管道饱和事故里保住了今天要用的营销看板（Day 103 场景 2），周六又把"上线前容量评审"写成了正式提案发进 QBR 邮件链（Day 104）。

现在最关键的人出现了：Growth 团队 lead Elena——她周六才第一次读到那封邮件，会前 10 分钟在咖啡角把你拦下。她不反对你的提案，但有一个条件：别在 VP 面前把她团队描述成"事故元凶"。你要在 25 分钟内和她对齐"讲什么、怎么讲、谁来讲"。

📖【完整对话】
─────────────────────────

**A (Leo, Data Platform):** Morning Elena. Got ten minutes before the QBR? I'd rather we walk in on the same page about Friday's incident and the capacity-review proposal.
（早上好 Elena。QBR 前有十分钟吗？关于周五的事故和容量评审提案，我希望我们进场时口径一致。）

**B (Elena, Growth Lead):** Ten minutes is all I've got, so let's make them count. I read your email Saturday. Solid write-up — very thorough. Maybe a little too thorough on who triggered the queue saturation.
（我就十分钟，所以得用在刀刃上。周六我读了你的邮件。写得很扎实——非常详尽。也许在"谁触发队列饱和"这块有点过于详尽了。）

**A (Leo):** Fair. To be clear, I never framed it as Growth's fault — the root cause section says the platform had no guardrails for event spikes. That was a gap on our side.
（明白。说清楚：我从没把它框定成 Growth 的错——根因部分写的是平台对事件激增没有任何护栏。那是我方的缺口。）

**B (Elena):** I know, and I appreciate that. But in the room, tone matters more than text. If the slide says "Growth's experiment drove 6x traffic," that's all the VP will remember.
（我知道，也正因如此我很感谢。但在会议室里，语气比文字更重要。如果幻灯片上写着"Growth 的实验带来 6 倍流量"，VP 记住的就只有这一句。）

**A (Leo):** Good point. How about this — we frame it as a shared systems issue: growth outpaced our visibility. That's factually true and it sets up the proposal naturally.
（有道理。这样如何——我们把它框定成一个共同的系统问题：增长速度超过了我们的可见性。这在事实上是真的，而且能自然引出提案。）

**B (Elena):** Now that's a framing I can get behind. And I'll say it myself — "our launches were the trigger, and we own our share of the fix." Volunteering reads better than being volunteered.
（这个框定我愿意支持。而且我自己来说——"我们的发布是触发点，修复中我们承担自己那份。"主动认领总比被点名认领好看。）

**A (Leo):** That would carry a lot more weight coming from you. For the pilot, is Growth still comfortable going first, or should we soften that in the room?
（这话从你嘴里说出来分量重得多。至于试点，Growth 还愿意第一个上吗，还是会上我们应该说得委婉一点？）

**B (Elena):** We're still in — Dev already told you we owe you one. But let's set expectations: lightweight checklist, async sign-off, and a two-week trial before anyone calls it a process.
（我们仍然参与——Dev 不是说了嘛，我们欠你一次。但先把预期定好：轻量清单、异步签核、两周试用，之后才谈要不要变成正式流程。）

**A (Leo):** That's exactly what the email says — ten minutes per launch, async. I'll add a bullet on the slide: "two-week pilot, review on Oct 2." You good with being named as pilot lead?
（邮件里正是这么写的——每次发布十分钟、异步。我在幻灯片上加一条："两周试点，10 月 2 日复盘。"写你为试点负责人，OK 吗？）

**B (Elena):** As long as it says "volunteered," not "assigned." Alright, five minutes left — walk me through the dashboard slide once. If the numbers are fresh, I've got no questions.
（只要写的是"主动请缨"而不是"被指定"就行。好，还剩五分钟——带我过一遍看板那页幻灯片。数据如果是新的，我就没问题了。）

**A (Leo):** All caught up as of 7 AM — Friday's catch-up held. In fact, I'd like to open with that: dashboards are fresh, pilot is staffed, and the fix already shipped. We lead with what went right.
（今早 7 点起全部追平——周五的追赶生效了。实际上我想用这个开场：看板数据是新的、试点有人了、修复已上线。我们先讲做对了的部分。）

**B (Elena):** Now you're thinking like a lead. Let's go — and Leo, nice work turning a Friday fire drill into a Monday process fix. No cap.
（你这就叫会用带头人的思路想问题了。走吧——还有 Leo，把周五的消防演习变成周一的流程修复，干得漂亮。不骗你。）

🎯【语块精讲】（8 + 1）
─────────────────────────

• **walk in on the same page** — 进场时口径一致/意见一致
  工作场景：Let's make sure sales and legal walk into the negotiation on the same page.
  日常场景：Before we meet the landlord, let's get on the same page about our budget.

• **make them count** — 让（有限的时间/机会）发挥最大价值
  工作场景：We only have ten minutes with the CEO, so make them count.
  日常场景：It's my last day in Tokyo — I'm going to make it count.

• **a little too thorough** — "有点过于详尽"——用 a little 软化的批评
  工作场景：The report was solid, maybe a little too thorough for a weekly update.
  日常场景：The movie review was fine, a little too thorough on the plot twists.

• **frame it as** — 把某事框定/描述成（某种性质）
  工作场景：Don't frame the delay as a failure — frame it as a trade-off for quality.
  日常场景：She framed quitting her job as a fresh start, not a retreat.

• **get behind** — 公开支持、拥护（某个想法/提议）
  工作场景：Once engineering explained the numbers, the whole leadership team got behind the plan.
  日常场景：I could get behind a road trip if we stop for BBQ halfway.

• **Volunteering reads better than being volunteered** — 主动认领比被点名更好看（reads = 给人的观感）
  工作场景：Offer to take notes in the kickoff — volunteering reads better than being volunteered.
  日常场景：Bring a dish to the potluck; volunteering always reads better.

• **carry a lot more weight** — 更有分量、更让人信服
  工作场景：The cost argument carries more weight when it comes from finance, not engineering.
  日常场景：Dad's advice hits different — it just carries more weight.

• **set expectations** — 提前对齐预期、把话说明白
  工作场景：Let me set expectations up front: we can deliver two of the three features by June.
  日常场景：Set expectations with the kids now — one hour of screens, no negotiating.

• 🎁 **彩蛋：No cap** — 真的、不骗你（Gen Z 俚语，Day 105 词典回归）
  工作场景：The new intern's deck was genuinely good. No cap.
  日常场景：That street taco was the best thing I've eaten all month, no cap.
