**场景一：公司的"创新委员会"提议被搁置了，你要把它救活**

你是委员会成员。半年前全员大会的头脑风暴产出了 40+ 条点子，躺在一张共享表里没人动。委员会最近要开季度会，你打算提案："把这张表救活。"用本周语块把提案说清楚。

**练习任务**：把下面这段中文腹稿的每个要点，用本周语块翻成英文（至少用到 7 个语块）：

- 这份表半年没人看，成了一张没人读的待办清单
- 40 条点子去重后真正的需求只有六七条（batch / duplicate in, don't lump in）
- 先摘低垂的果实：三条两周内能见效的先做（low-hanging fruit）
- 明确现在承诺什么、不承诺什么（承诺边界）
- 热度早凉了，但结构可以留下：每个点子挂一个 owner、一个季度（scaffolding / now that the dust has settled）
- 委员会不需要来一条审一条，每周一次 sweep 就够了（sweep / runs on its own）

**参考答案**：

> "Now that the dust has settled from the all-hands, can we talk about the ideas sheet? Half a year in, it's become a to-do list nobody reads. I went through all forty entries — batched them, deduplicated them, and there are maybe six real ideas in there. So let's duplicate them in, not lump them in; each one gets an owner and a quarter. Three of them are low-hanging fruit — two weeks of work, visible to everyone, which is exactly how we rebuild trust in this sheet. To be clear about commitment: we're committing to triage every quarter, not to build everything on the list. The sheet was scaffolding for the all-hands energy; if we give it a weekly sweep — ten minutes, one owner — it can run on its own. What we shouldn't do is let it die of 'quick look' requests for 'just a small prototype.'"

（尘埃落定之后，能聊聊点子表吗？半年了，它已经变成一张没人读的待办清单。我把四十条全过了一遍——打了包、去了重，真正的点子大概只有六条。所以：疑似重复先登记，别提前打包；每条挂一个 owner、一个季度。其中三条是低垂果实——两周工作量、全员可见，正好用它重建大家对这张表的信任。承诺边界说清楚：我们承诺的是每季度分诊，不是表上的都做。这张表是大会热度的脚手架；每周十分钟、一个 owner 的 sweep，它就能自己转。唯一不能做的，就是让它死于无数个"就做个小 demo 看看"的顺嘴请求。）

**场景二：家庭群 500 人，相亲角、拼多多、养生谣言混着来，你是新上任的群主**

上任第一周，你要发一条置顶公告。**练习任务**：用本周语块写公告正文（英文，至少 5 个语块），要求"改管道不拒人"——每条规矩都告诉家人"正确的家在哪里"。

**参考答案**：

> 📌 **Pinned: How this family group runs now**
>
> Hi all — now that the dust has settled on my taking over as admin, three house rules. The @all button is reserved for birthdays and emergencies — everything else reaches us just as fast as a normal message. (Yes, even the coupon links.) The group chat isn't going away, but it's graduating: buying links go in the "Deals" thread, health articles go in the "Fact-check" thread where cousin Ming volunteers to verify them, and the matchmaking photos get their own thread, where nobody has to pretend they didn't see them. Redirecting the pipe, not rejecting the person — we love your content, we just gave each kind a home. Every Sunday night I'll do a five-minute sweep and post what's new. That's it. The group basically runs on its own after that, and nobody's Sunday dinner gets interrupted by 200 unread messages.

（大家好——尘埃落定、我接任群主，三条家规。@全体只留给生日和急事——其他消息普通发送一样秒到。（对，包括优惠券链接。）群不关停，但要"毕业"：拼团链接进"团购"话题、养生文章进"辟谣"话题（表弟明自愿核验）、相亲照片单独开话题——没人需要假装没看见。改管道，不是拒绝人——我们爱你们的内容，只是给每类内容安了个家。每周日晚我花五分钟 sweep 一次、发一条本周新事。就这样。之后这个群基本自己运转，也没人的周日晚饭再被 200 条未读打断。）

**场景三：你第一周当兼职登山队领队，出发前一晚队员狂发消息，你慌了，找老领队 sanity check**

**练习任务**：先自己写 6-8 句"求助+分诊"对话（A=你，B=老领队），必须用到 sanity check / fire away / treat it like one / lead with X, not Y，然后再看参考答案。

**参考答案**：

> **A (you):** Pat, quick sanity check before I combust? First time leading, and my phone hasn't stopped since 8 p.m. Fire away if you have five minutes.
> Pat，快速对个口径，不然我要炸了？第一次带队，晚八点起手机就没停过。有五分钟的话尽管来。
>
> **B (Pat, veteran leader):** Fire away. What's in the pile?
> 说。堆里都是什么？
>
> **A:** One teammate says his boots fell apart — like, literally, sole detached. Two people asking about the weather. One asking if her friend can tag along. And one message that just says "what time tomorrow?" with no context.
> 一个队友说登山靴开胶了——真的，鞋底掉了。两个人问天气。一个问能不能带朋友。还有一条只有一句"明天几点？"上下文全无。
>
> **B:** Three buckets. The boots are the P1 — treat it like one: you can't verify his gear from your couch, so assume it's real and lead with the help, not the process. "Check the group-gear list — we carry a spare pair in size 43; you're covered." Weather questions: point people to the forecast link and the one line that matters — "rain jacket mandatory, we go rain or shine." The tag-along friend is a pipe redirect: "She's welcome on the public trip in two weeks; this one's a closed group." And "what time tomorrow" gets one reply in the pinned note — not a DM to you.
> 三个桶。靴子是 P1——按 P1 对待：你没法在沙发上验证他的装备，所以默认是真的，而且先给帮助、再讲流程。"看下公共装备清单——我们带了一双 43 码备用，你有保障。"天气问题：把人指向预报链接和唯一重要的那句话——"冲锋衣必带，风雨无阻。"带朋友是改管道："两周后的公开活动欢迎她；这次是封闭小队。"至于"明天几点"——置顶公告里回一次，不私信回你。
>
> **A:** That's… the whole pile, handled in four moves. Anything I'm missing?
> 这就……四步清完了。还有什么漏的？
>
> **B:** One thing: after tonight, post a one-line digest — "Boots: covered. Weather: link pinned. Guest: redirected. Time: see pinned note." Then put the phone down. Trust is built in the receipts — you give the receipts, the pile stops growing.
> 一件事：今晚睡前发一行摘要——"靴子：已解决。天气：链接已置顶。带人：已改道。时间：看置顶。"然后放下手机。信任靠回执建立——回执发出去，堆就不再长了。

**题 1：公开评论区全大写怒吼"系统吞了我的订单！！！"——先帮后规矩的公开回复**

> "So sorry about the missing order — that's the worst thing an order can do, and we're treating it like a top-priority case even though we can't see it on our side yet. I've opened ticket #412 and DM'd you three quick questions (order date, payment method, and the email you used), and we'll trace it from there. For future reference, a regular reply here reaches us just as fast as all-caps — we read every single one."
>
> 非常抱歉订单不见了——这是订单能出的最糟状况，哪怕我们这边暂时查不到，也按最高优先级处理。已开工单 #412，私信发你三个问题（下单日期、支付方式、所用邮箱），我们从那里开始追。友情提示：普通回复和全大写一样秒达——每一条我们都看。

**题 2：知识库"提问区"混进真提问/会议纪要/吐槽——友善置顶公告**

> 📌 **Pinned: What lives in the Q&A space**
>
> Hi all! Quick housekeeping so this space stays useful. This Q&A area is reserved for questions — the kind with an answer we can all find later. Meeting notes have graduated to the Docs area (same content, better search). And venting? We hear you — the #watercooler channel is where it belongs, and honestly, it's livelier there anyway. Redirecting, not rejecting: every kind of post now has a home, and future-you doing a search will thank present-you.
>
> 大家好！小规矩，让这个空间保持有用。提问区专用于提问——就是那种答案日后大家都能搜到的问题。会议纪要已经"毕业"去文档区（内容一样，搜索更方便）。吐槽？我们听到了——它属于 #watercooler 频道，而且说实话在那边更有生气。改管道，不拒人：每类帖子现在都有自己的家，未来搜索的你会感谢现在发帖的你。

**题 3：伴娘总管排练日三大爆点——10 分钟分诊教学**

> "Breathe. Sanity check incoming: three problems, three different sizes. The flowers being the wrong color is the P1 — treat it like one: the wedding is in 48 hours, so you call the florist NOW and ask what they can do by Friday noon; photos last forever, apologies don't. The sound system is not confirmed — that's the fixable one: one text to the venue, 'Confirming AV for Saturday 4 p.m. — reply YES.' Triage it as urgent-but-one-action. The flower girl might be late — that's a duplicate-in, not a lump-in: note it, give mom a 30-minute-early call time, and let it go. If she's late, we adjust; the ceremony doesn't start without you anyway. Now post the one-line receipt in the bridal chat — 'Florist: called. AV: texted. Flower girl: early call time set' — and go eat something. Trust is built in the receipts."
>
> 呼吸。口径检查来了：三个问题，三个尺码。花艺颜色错了是 P1——按 P1 对待：婚礼只剩 48 小时，现在就打给花艺师，问周五中午前能做什么；照片是一辈子的事，道歉不是。音响没确认——这是能修的那种：给场地发一条短信"确认周六下午 4 点 AV——回个 YES。"分诊为"紧急但只需一个动作"。花童可能迟到——这条 duplicate in，别 lump in：记下来、给妈妈设提前 30 分钟的集合时间、然后放手。真迟到了我们调整，反正典礼没你也开不了场。现在去新娘群发一行回执——"花艺：已打。音响：已发。花童：已设早到"——然后去吃点东西。信任是靠回执建立的。

**"Trust is built in the receipts."**
（信任是靠回执建立的。）

这句话来自 Day 121，值得再贴一周。频道的信任、家庭的信任、团队的信任，全都是同一件事：对方看不见你做了多少，只看得见你有没有"接住"的那一下。一行 sweep 摘要、一条"已开工单"、一个"链接已置顶"——三十秒的回执，胜过十分钟的解释。今天挑一件你在"值班"的事，给它发一张回执。
