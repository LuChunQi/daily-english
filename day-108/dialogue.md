🎯【语块精讲】（8 + 1）
─────────────────────────

• **working from the same page** — 对着同一份事实工作（不是各凭记忆/各说各话）
  工作场景：The spec changed twice today — let me post the final version so we're all working from the same page.
  日常场景：We told Grandma the party's at 6 and told Dad 7:30. We are NOT working from the same page.

• **get ahead of (something)** — 提前处理、抢在（问题/传言）发生之前
  工作场景：Let's get ahead of the layoff rumors with an all-hands Q&A.
  日常场景：Get ahead of the jet lag — start sleeping on the plane's schedule three days early.

• **the rumor mill** — 传言机器（办公室小道消息的自循环）
  工作场景：Before the rumor mill fills the silence, here's what actually happened with the reorg.
  日常场景：The rumor mill says they broke up — or maybe he just changed his profile pic. Hard to say.

• **lead with** — 用……开场（决定受众第一印象的信息排序）
  工作场景：Lead with the outcome, not the debugging journey — execs read the first two lines only.
  日常场景：When you meet her parents, lead with the fact you're a doctor, not that you ride a motorcycle.

• **nothing mandatory until the numbers earn it** — 数据说话之前不做强制（试点期自愿原则的地道表达）
  工作场景：The new checklist is optional for this quarter — nothing mandatory until the numbers earn it.
  日常场景：We're testing the new chore chart gently — nothing mandatory until the kids actually like it.

• **becomes a formality, not a defense** — 从"辩护"变"走过场"（提前沟通的红利）
  工作场景：Keep the weekly updates flowing, and the renewal meeting becomes a formality, not a defense.
  日常场景：Text your mom every Sunday, and the holiday visit becomes a formality, not an interrogation.

• **flag it here first, not after** — 先亮出来，别等事后（变更透明的 Slack 姿态）
  工作场景：If the launch date moves again, I'll flag it in this channel first — not after the tickets pile up.
  日常场景：If I'm running late again, I'll text you first, not after you've been standing there 20 minutes.

• **bring (something) to the table** — 拿出（谈判筹码/价值/优势）（之前提到——正式确认）
  工作场景：Each team brings something to the table: data, traffic, or budget.
  日常场景：He brings loyalty and terrible dancing to the table — a fair trade, honestly.

• 🎁 **彩蛋：a formality, not a defense** — 同上第 6 条，值得单独记：**所有提前沟通的最终奖励，是让未来的关键会议从"辩护席"挪到"签字席"。** 你不是在避免冲突，你是在预付信任。

**题 1：走廊 30 秒回答"迁移项目还要不要继续"**（What's the ask? 思维：一句话结论 + 一个明确请求）
> "Yes, we continue — the first phase paid for itself already. What I need from you today is one thing: a yes or no on extending the deadline two weeks."

**题 2：劝朋友买旅行保险**（run the numbers / a small price to pay）
> "I ran the numbers — the insurance is 3% of the trip cost, while one cancelled flight would eat 60% of it. Three percent for total peace of mind? That's a small price to pay."

**题 3：让 code review 新规撑过三周**（make it stick / rubber-stamp）
> "For this to stick, it can't be something the leads rubber-stamped in a meeting. Let's build it into the merge checklist so it runs automatically, and review the numbers at the retro — kill it if it's not working."
