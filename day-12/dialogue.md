### 📝 场景 1 参考答案

**Slack 消息：**

> 🚨 **@here** — Just **flagging** that we have a **production incident** on the payment service. Error rate spiked to 15% in the last 20 minutes. I've **pulled in** the on-call team and we're investigating. Will share updates in this thread every 30 minutes.

> @Lisa @Tom — I'm currently **blocked on** the database logs. Can someone **nudge** the DBA team to grant access? A **gentle reminder** that this is P1.

> Quick **check-in** — we've identified the root cause (bad config deploy). We're **assessing the impact** and expect a fix within the hour. Will **escalate** to the VP if we're not resolved by 9 PM.

**邮件正文：**

> **Subject: Production Incident — Payment Service (Resolved)**
>
> Team,
>
> We experienced a **production incident** on the payment service tonight from 8:00 to 8:45 PM. Root cause: a misconfigured parameter in the latest deploy.
>
> **What happened:** Error rate spiked to 15% affecting approximately 200 transactions.
>
> **What we did:** On-call team identified and reverted the bad config within 45 minutes.
>
> **Impact:** We're still **assessing the impact** on downstream systems, but the service is now stable. All **milestones remain on track** for the quarterly release.
>
> **Next steps:** We're adding a config validation check to the CI pipeline. Will share the post-mortem by EOD Friday — please **sign off** on the action items when you receive it.
>
> Let me know if you have questions.

### 📝 场景 2 参考答案

**A — Lisa (PM)**
**B — 你 (Tech Lead)**

---

A (Lisa): I think we should delay the launch by two weeks. The QA team is still finding bugs, and I don't want to risk another production incident.
我觉得我们应该把上线推迟两周。QA 还在发现 bug，我不想冒险再出一次线上故障。

B (你): **I see where you're coming from**, Lisa. Quality is definitely the priority. But **let me push back** a bit — we've already delayed twice, and the stakeholders are getting restless.
我理解你的考虑，Lisa。质量确实是优先级。但让我提一下异议——我们已经推迟两次了，利益相关方开始坐不住了。

A (Lisa): **Fair point.** But wouldn't you rather ship late than ship broken?
有道理。但你宁愿晚发也不愿意发一个有问题的版本吧？

B (你): Of course. But **playing devil's advocate** — if we wait until there are zero bugs, we'll never launch. The current bugs are all P2 and below.
当然。但我来唱个反调——如果我们等到零 bug 才发，就永远发不了了。目前的 bug 都是 P2 及以下的。

A (Lisa): OK, **I'll hear you out.** What's your proposal?
好，我听你说完。你的方案是什么？

B (你): What if we launch on time but with a reduced feature set? We cut the two lowest-priority features, ship the stable core, and add the rest in a patch release the following week.
如果我们按时上线但缩减功能集呢？砍掉优先级最低的两个功能，发稳定的内核，剩下的下周补丁版本加上。

A (Lisa): Hmm, that could work. But I'd need the QA lead to **sign off** on the reduced scope.
嗯，这个可以。但我需要 QA 负责人批准缩减后的范围。

B (你): Agreed. Let's **nudge** him for a quick review tomorrow morning, and we can **make the call** by noon. Sound good?
同意。我们明天早上催他快速 review 一下，中午前就能做决定。怎么样？

A (Lisa): Sounds like a plan. Thanks for not just saying "no" — it's good to **hear each other out** before deciding.
就这么定了。谢谢你不止是说"不行"——决定前互相听对方说完挺好的。

### 📝 场景 3 参考答案

**A — Jake (Canadian colleague)**
**B — 你**

---

A (Jake): Hey! A bunch of us are heading to that new pho place for lunch. **I wouldn't say no to** some company — want to join?
嘿！我们几个要去那家新开的越南粉店吃午饭。我挺想有人一起的——要来吗？

B (你): Man, I've been **swamped** all morning. I got **pulled into** a surprise code review session that ate up two hours.
天，我一上午忙得不可开交。被拉进一个突发的代码评审，直接吃了两小时。

A (Jake): Ouch. Sounds like you need to **recover from the week** and it's only Thursday.
哎。听起来你需要从这周恢复一下，而且今天才周四。

B (你): Tell me about it. But you know what — **I wouldn't say no to** a bowl of hot pho right now.
可不是嘛。不过你知道吗——我现在不会拒绝一碗热腾腾的越南粉。

A (Jake): That's the spirit! **Fair warning** though — I heard their extra spicy is no joke.
这就对了！不过提前提醒你——听说他们的特辣可不是闹着玩的。

B (你): **Challenge accepted!** I eat mala xiangguo every week — I think I can handle it.
挑战接受！我每周都吃麻辣香锅——我觉得我搞得定。

A (Jake): Alright, let's go before you change your mind. It's also a good chance for a **quick check-in** on the design project.
好，趁你没改主意赶紧走。也是个快速同步一下设计项目的好机会。
