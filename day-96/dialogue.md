### 🎬 场景 1：休假一周回来，桌上堆了四件事（工作 · 排期 + 拍板）

**背景：** 你休假一周刚回来，周一早上 9:40，Team Lead Rachel 在茶水间"抓"到你，要在站会前把本周优先级对齐——顺便把一个悬了三周的日志方案问题当场定下来。

---

**A (Rachel, Team Lead):** Welcome back! Looks rested. Before standup piles up — can we do a quick sync on your week?
> 欢迎回来！气色不错。趁站会还没堆起来——快速对一下你这周的计划？

**B (你):** Sure. Fair to say I've got a lot on my plate after a week away: the log pipeline migration, Sam's alerting handover — he left while I was out — and an API doc review.
> 行。休假一周回来，手上的事可以说不少了：日志管道迁移、Sam 的告警系统交接——我休假时他离职了——还有一个 API 文档评审。

**A (Rachel):** That's three. Which one's blocking other people?
> 三件了。哪件在阻塞别人？

**B (你):** The log pipeline, definitely — the data team can't build dashboards until it's done.
> 肯定是日志管道——它不完成，数据团队的仪表盘就没法做。

**A (Rachel):** Then that's your headline for the week. The doc review — did anyone actually commit to a deadline?
> 那它就是你本周的头等大事。文档评审——真有人给出确定截止日期了吗？

**B (你):** Of course not. "The sooner the better."
> 当然没有。"越快越好。"

**A (Rachel):** Classic. Push back on it and ask for a real date — if they can't give one, it waits. What about the handover?
> 太经典了。推回去要个确切日期——给不出来就先放着。交接的事呢？

**B (你):** I'd like to carve out Tuesday morning for it, while the context is still fresh in Sam's exit notes.
> 我想周二上午专门留给它，趁 Sam 的离职交接笔记还新鲜。

**A (Rachel):** Good. Now — the logging tool question. It's been hanging for three weeks, and it's quietly blocking your migration. Let's settle it right here. Lay out the options for me.
> 好。那——日志工具的选型问题。已经悬了三周，而且它在暗暗阻塞你的迁移。就在这儿定了吧。把选项给我摆出来。

**B (你):** Two options. Option A: stay self-hosted on ELK — we know it, but maintenance eats two days a month. Option B: move to managed Loki — almost zero maintenance, but a three-week migration. The trade-off is engineering time versus migration risk.
> 两个选项。A：继续自建 ELK——我们熟，但每月维护要吃掉两天。B：迁到托管 Loki——维护几乎为零，但迁移要三周。权衡就是工程时间和迁移风险二选一。

**A (Rachel):** Worst case?
> 最坏情况呢？

**B (你):** Worst case, we're halfway into the migration, hit something ugly, and roll back — we lose two weeks and stay on ELK.
> 最坏情况，迁移做到一半撞上硬骨头，回滚——损失两周，继续用 ELK。

**A (Rachel):** Can we hedge our bets — run Loki in parallel for the new pipeline while ELK keeps the old one?
> 能不能两头下注——新管道跑 Loki，老的先用 ELK 顶着？

**B (你):** Yes, actually — the migration design already supports that. Two weeks of double-running, then we decide with real numbers.
> 可以，其实——迁移设计本身就支持这个。并行跑两周，然后拿真实数据来决定。

**A (Rachel):** Perfect. Let's call it: Loki in parallel, decide in two weeks. I'll back you in standup — I want everyone on the same page before you start.
> 完美。就这么定：Loki 并行跑，两周后定夺。站会上我给你背书——在你开工前让所有人信息同步。

**B (你):** Works for me. Logging first, handover Tuesday, doc review pending a real date.
> 我没问题。日志优先，交接周二，文档评审等确切日期。

### 🎬 场景 2：周四上午，code freeze 中午就到（工作 · Slack 催办）

**背景：** 周四上午 10 点。你接手告警系统后改了个 PR，卡在 Senior Engineer Maya 的 review 上——中午 12 点 code freeze。你在 Slack 上私聊她。顺便要提一件大家都没明说的事。

---

**A (你):** Hi Maya — just a quick check-in on PR #512, the alerting fix. The code freeze is at noon today, so that's kind of a drop-dead date. Any chance you could take a look this morning?
> 嗨 Maya——快速跟进一下 PR #512，告警修复那个。今天中午 12 点代码冻结，算是条死线了。上午方便看一眼吗？

**B (Maya):** Ugh, it completely slipped my mind — sorry! I was heads-down on release notes. If I start in an hour, is that cutting it too close?
> 啊，这事整个从我脑子里滑掉了——抱歉！我一直在埋头写发布说明。一小时后开始看，会不会太赶？

**A (你):** An hour works. Most of it is config changes — feel free to skim. The only tricky part is section three, the escalation rules.
> 一小时后没问题。大部分是配置变更——随便扫一眼就行。唯一棘手的是第三节，升级规则那块。

**B (Maya):** That'd be a lifesaver — I've got three other reviews queued. Actually, while I have you: can I say something? The alerting runbook hasn't been updated since last year, and nobody's owned it since Sam left. I think that's the elephant in the room.
> 那真是救命——我后面还排着三个 review。其实正好，有句话能说吗？告警手册从去年就没更新过，Sam 走了之后更是没人认领。我觉得这就是屋里那头大象。

**A (你):** Honestly? Yes. Stuff keeps falling through the cracks precisely because no one owns the docs. I hit three outdated pages just this week.
> 说实话？是的。事情老是从缝里漏掉，恰恰就是因为文档没人认领。光这周我就撞见三页过时的。

**B (Maya):** Well, now it's on the table. Should we bite the bullet and rewrite it next sprint? I can draft the skeleton — new doc, up and running within a week, then the team fills in the gaps.
> 呃，现在既然摆上桌面了。下个 sprint 要不要咬牙把它重写了？我可以搭骨架——新文档一周内立起来跑通，然后团队各自填坑。

**A (你):** I'd love that. I'll raise ownership at the leads' meeting so it doesn't all land on you.
> 求之不得。认领权的事我去组长会上提，别全压你一个人身上。

**B (Maya):** Perfect. And hey — next time don't wait two days. Just ping me after 24 hours, I won't take it personally.
> 完美。还有——下次别等两天。24 小时后直接 ping 我，我不会介意的。

**A (你):** Deal. Review in an hour — thanks, Maya.
> 成交。一小时后见 review——谢了 Maya。

**📝 本场景使用语块（13个）：**
just a quick check-in on / slip my mind / code freeze / drop-dead date / cutting it too close / feel free to skim it / That'd be a lifesaver / the elephant in the room / fall through the cracks / on the table / bite the bullet / up and running / just ping me 🎁

### 🎬 场景 3：周五傍晚 welcome drinks，欢迎新同事（社交 · 破冰）

**背景：** 周五下班，团队在楼下酒馆为新入职的 Grace 和 Jonas 搞了个小型 welcome drinks。你是团队老员工，端着饮料走过去。

---

**A (你):** Hey! Great to finally put faces to names — I've seen you both all over the all-hands deck. How are the first few weeks treating you?
> 嘿！终于把名字和脸对上号了，真好——全员大会的 PPT 里老见你们俩。这几周待你如何？

**B (Grace, New PM):** Honestly, it's a lot to take in — new domain, new tools, new acronyms every day. I've basically survived on the onboarding wiki and far too much coffee.
> 说实话，要吸收的东西太多了——新领域、新工具、每天还有新缩写。我基本靠入职维基和过量咖啡续命。

**C (Jonas, New Engineer):** Guilty as charged on the wiki part — I read the payments runbook cover to cover last weekend. Twice, actually.
> 维基那部分我认罪——上周末我把支付手册从头到尾读了一遍。准确说，两遍。

**A (你):** Twice? That's more than most of the team has ever read it. Seriously though — you two have fresh eyes. If anything in that runbook is wrong or outdated, bring it to me. I'd rather fix ten doc bugs now than debug one mystery in production later.
> 两遍？比团队大多数人都读得多了。说真的——你们俩可是新鲜视角。手册里要是有错或过时，直接找我。我宁愿现在修十个文档 bug，也不想以后在生产环境调试一个谜团。

**B (Grace):** Deal — I already have three pages of notes.
> 成交——我已经记了三页笔记了。

**A (你):** Bring them on! Oh, and by the way, Jonas — your first-week summary made it all the way to the VP's radar. She quoted your "three quick wins" slide at the staff meeting.
> 尽管来！哦对了 Jonas——你第一周的那份总结都传到 VP 耳朵里了。她在管理层会议上还引用了你那页"三个速赢"的片子。

**C (Jonas):** No way. I thought nobody read those!
> 不会吧。我还以为没人看那个呢！

**A (你):** True story. OK — first round is on me tonight. Welcome to the team, both of you.
> 千真万确。好了——今晚第一轮我请。欢迎加入团队，二位。

**B (Grace):** That's very kind. Same time next week?
> 你太客气了。下周同一时间？

**A (你):** Careful — that's how traditions start.
> 小心——传统就是这么开始的。
