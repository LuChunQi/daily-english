### 🎬 场景 1：六周后，你站在知识分享会的讲台上（工作 · 知识传承）

**背景：** On-call 顺利结束，你把接手的系统摸熟了。公司工程公会请你做一场 15 分钟分享：《如何接手一个没人写文档的系统》。台下坐着 Priya——她下周就要接手一位离职同事的推荐系统。

---

**A (You, sharing the experience):** Thanks for having me. Six weeks ago a colleague left the company, and I picked up where he left off — a core system where the docs covered the happy path and not much else. First week honestly felt like drinking from a firehose.
> 谢谢邀请。六周前有位同事离职，我接手了他留下的摊子——一个核心系统，文档只写了正常流程，别的几乎没写。第一周说实话像对着消防水管喝水。

**B (Priya, about to take over a system):** That's exactly my situation. The engineer leaving my team owns the recommendation pipeline — under the hood, nobody else knows how it works.
> 我这情况一模一样。我们组要走的那位独占着推荐管道——皮底下的东西，其他没人知道怎么运转。

**A (You):** Then my first tip: before he leaves, pick his brain — book thirty minutes a day, and don't just ask "how does it work?" Ask "what would break this?"
> 那我的第一条建议：趁他还没走赶紧请教——每天约三十分钟，别光问"它是怎么跑的"，要问"什么会把它搞挂？"

**B (Priya):** Smart. How long did the hardest part take you?
> 聪明。最难的部分你花了多久？

**A (You):** The refund flow took me a full week to wrap my head around — every state transition, every failure mode. Worth every hour, because after that I could answer questions in the weekly meeting instead of going quiet.
> 退款流程我花了整整一周才彻底搞明白——每个状态流转、每种失败模式。每个小时都值，因为从那以后周会上我能接得住问题，而不是沉默装死。

**B (Priya):** And how do you avoid becoming the single point of failure yourself, one handover later?
> 那你怎么避免自己一轮交接之后也变成新的单点故障？

**A (You):** Document as you go. Every time something surprises you, write it down the same day — messy notes beat perfect notes you never write. That's how the March incident finally made it into our runbook.
> 边做边记。每次被什么东西惊到，当天就写下来——乱糟糟的笔记也胜过永远不写的完美笔记。三月那次事故就是这么终于进了我们的手册。

**B (Priya):** On that — any tips for the politics? Getting time for documentation is always the fight.
> 说到这个——"政治"上有什么技巧吗？挤出写文档的时间永远是场斗争。

**A (You):** Leave a paper trail. When I flagged the risk in email and my manager replied "take Fridays," that thread became the commitment. Verbal promises evaporate; written ones stick.
> 留书面痕迹。我把风险写进邮件、经理回复"周五归你"之后，那封往来就成了承诺。口头承诺会蒸发，书面的能粘住。

**B (Priya):** Last one — how long until you felt you actually owned it?
> 最后一个——多久之后你才觉得真正拥有它了？

**A (You):** Honestly, the nightly jobs still scare me a little — I'm not out of the woods there. But the pager? After one baptism-by-fire week, I was fielding questions in the weekly sync and realized I knew the system inside out.
> 说实话，每晚的批处理还是让我有点怵——那块我还没脱离险区。但 pager？经过一周火线洗礼，周会上我现场接问题的时候突然意识到：这系统我已经摸得里外通透了。

**B (Priya):** This was gold. We're spinning up a similar handover next month — would you run point on a shared takeover playbook for both teams?
> 太值了。我们下个月也要启动类似的交接——你愿意牵头做一份两个团队共用的"接手手册"吗？

**A (You):** I'll take that one — count me in. Let's grab time next week.
> 这个我来接——算我一个。下周约时间。

### 🎬 场景 2：周五下午四点，你又被告警叫醒（工作 · 排障 + 责任分摊）

**背景：** 两个月后的季度末周五。你刚被 page——营销看板数据全过期了。追查发现是增长团队今早上线的实验平台把事件量打爆了队列。你在 Slack huddle 里找到对方工程师 Dev。

---

**A (You, on-call owner):** Dev, got a sec? I just got paged — the marketing dashboards are three hours stale. Nothing user-facing yet, but Monday's QBR deck pulls from them.
> Dev，有空吗？我刚被 page 了——营销看板的数据过期三小时了。还没有影响面向用户的功能，但周一的季度汇报 PPT 要从里面取数。

**B (Dev, Growth team engineer):** Oh no, that might be us. We launched the experiment platform this morning — is it hammering your queue?
> 糟糕，可能是我们。今早上了实验平台——是不是把你们的队列打爆了？

**A (You):** I just traced it back — your events are six times the usual volume. The queue had headroom for maybe two x, not six.
> 我刚追溯过去了——你们的事件量是平时的六倍。队列的余量只够吃下两倍，吃不下六倍。

**B (Dev):** I'm so sorry — I thought the volume estimates went out in the launch email. My bad.
> 实在抱歉——我以为容量预估在发布邮件里说过了。我的锅。

**A (You):** Honestly, that's on the docs as much as on you — the queue limits were never written down anywhere. I only learned them by getting paged.
> 说实话，这事儿一半怪文档一半怪你——队列上限从来没写在任何地方。我也是被 page 了才知道的。

**B (Dev):** Ugh. What do you need from me? I can throttle the experiments back right now.
> 呃。需要我做什么？我现在就能把实验降速。

**A (You):** Dial it down to thirty percent until the backlog drains. The queue catches up at about five thousand events a minute, so by seven you should be clear — then resume tonight.
> 降到 30%，等积压清完。队列每分钟消化五千条，七点前应该追平——今晚再恢复全速。

**B (Dev):** Done. And the dashboards will be fresh before Monday's QBR?
> 搞定。那周一 QBR 前看板数据会是新的吧？

**A (You):** Long story short, yes — as long as nobody stacks another fire on top. Which is why I want one thing going forward.
> 长话短说，能——只要别再有人往上摞一把火。所以我往后要求一件事。

**B (Dev):** Name it.
> 说。

**A (You):** A capacity review before any launch that pushes more than two x volume. Not a big meeting — a shared doc, a realistic timeline, sign-off in writing. If it's just a verbal "should be fine," I'm the one putting out fires at 4 PM on Fridays.
> 任何推送量超过两倍的发布，上线前先做容量评审。不用大会议——一份共享文档、一个现实的时间表、书面签核。如果只是口头一句"应该没事"，那周五下午四点救火的就是我。

**B (Dev):** Fair. I'll take it to our lead — and for what it's worth, you'll have my team's back on this. We owe you after today.
> 有道理。我带回给我们 lead——而且说真的，这事我们团队会站在你这边。今天之后我们欠你一次。

**A (You):** Appreciated. Now if you'll excuse me — I have a queue to watch drain.
> 谢了。失陪一下——我得去盯着队列排空了。

**📝 本场景使用语块（14个）：**
get paged / stale / user-facing / trace (it) back to / headroom / throttle back / dial it down / drain / catch up / that's on X as much as on Y / long story short / a realistic timeline / put out fires / have someone's back

### 🎬 场景 3：晚上七点，队列清空，taco 时间（社交 · 犒赏 + 战友闲聊）

**背景：** 晚七点，队列追平、看板全绿。你正要收拾回家，Maya（Day 102 那位 on-call 老兵）出现在你工位旁。

---

**A (Maya):** The queue's drained, the dashboards are green, and it's Friday night. Take a load off — there's a taco place two blocks out. You've earned it.
> 队列清空了，看板全绿了，今晚是周五。歇口气吧——两个街区外有家 taco 店。这是你应得的。

**B (You):** Ha! Old habit — I've been glued to my phone since four, half-expecting it to buzz again.
> 哈！老习惯了——四点起我就手机不离手，总觉得它随时会再响。

**A (Maya):** A six-x traffic spike with zero warning? You're in good company — half the incidents in this company start with "nobody knew the limit."
> 六倍流量零预警？你不是一个人——这公司一半的事故都始于"没人知道上限在哪"。

**B (You):** Comforting and terrifying at the same time.
> 又安心又害怕，两种感受同时存在。

**A (Maya):** That's the job. My first on-call quarter, I checked the dashboards at my own wedding rehearsal dinner. Rookie mistake.
> 这就是这份工作。我第一个 on-call 季度，在自己婚礼彩排晚宴上还在看仪表盘。新手才会犯的错。

**B (You):** Please tell me someone took a photo.
> 告诉我有人拍了照。

**A (Maya):** Three. They live in the team album now — prime material for trading war stories at parties.
> 三张。现在都躺在团队相册里——聚会互吐苦水的顶级素材。

**B (You):** Well, tonight's six-x story just made it into that album too. Not bad for someone who took over the system eight weeks ago.
> 呃，今晚的"六倍流量"故事也刚进了那本相册。对一个八周前才接手这系统的人来说不算差。

**A (Maya):** Not bad at all. Not everyone survives their first quarter — and lives to tell the tale with a taco in hand.
> 一点不差。可不是谁都能第一轮活下来——还能手里拿着 taco 讲这段历史。

**B (You):** Ha! By the way — since you found the taco place, this one's on me. Celebration tacos and splitting the bill just don't go together.
> 哈！对了——既然是你找到的店，这顿我来。庆祝用的 taco 再 AA 就太不搭了。

**A (Maya):** Fine, but only because it's a celebration — normally we'd split the bill, no fighting over it like in China.
> 行吧，但只因为今天是庆祝——平时我们可是 AA 的，可不能像在中国那样抢账单。

**B (You):** Deal. And no shop talk tonight — I need to take my mind off that queue.
> 成交。今晚不聊工作——我得把那条队列从脑子里赶出去。

**A (Maya):** Agreed. Monday's problems are Monday's. Tonight: tacos.
> 同意。周一的问题留给周一。今晚：taco。

**📝 本场景使用语块（9个）：**

1. **先盲听音频**，再对照速查表过一遍 36 个语块，标记"眼熟但说不出口"的那几个
2. **看三个新场景怎么串的**：场景 1→2→3 正好是"传承 → 救火 → 犒赏"的完整弧线，和本周 Day 99-102 的主线一一呼应——同一批语块换场景完全不违和，就是"据为己有"的标志
3. **本周四个万能句**，建议背到脱口而出：
   - "Can I pick your brain on ___?"（请教开场白，职场最高性价比的一句话）
   - "Long story short, ..."（汇报神器：先给结论，再展开）
   - "That's on the docs as much as on you."（责任分摊不伤人，批评也留余地）
   - "You've earned it."（人情存款：犒劳同事、肯定付出，一句顶十句）
4. **注意彩蛋句的活用**：make it into → "made it into the runbook / the album / the final cut"；I'll take that one → 会议抢答立 ownership；live to tell the tale → 自嘲式庆祝
